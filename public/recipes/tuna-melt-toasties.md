# Tuna Melt Toasties
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, family, easy

Golden, crispy toasties filled with tuna mayo and melted cheese. A cheap and speedy lunch.

## Ingredients
- 2 tins tuna (145g each), drained
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 8 slices bread
- 100g cheddar cheese, grated
- 25g butter, softened
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mix the tuna, mayonnaise, spring onions and some black pepper.
2. Spread the tuna over 4 slices of bread, top with tomato and cheese, then the remaining bread.
3. Butter the outsides and cook in a frying pan over a medium heat for 3–4 minutes per side, pressing down, until golden and the cheese has melted.

## Notes
- A sandwich toaster or grill works too.
