# Berry & Banana Breakfast Smoothie
Serves: 2
Time: 5 min
Tags: breakfast, quick, easy, vegetarian

A thick, filling smoothie with frozen berries, banana, yoghurt and oats. Breakfast in a glass.

## Ingredients
- 2 bananas
- 200g frozen berries
- 150g Greek yoghurt
- 300ml milk
- 30g porridge oats
- 1 tbsp honey

## Method
1. Put everything in a blender and blitz until smooth.
2. Add a splash more milk if it's too thick, then pour into glasses.

## Notes
- Peel, slice and freeze overripe bananas to use in smoothies.
