# Savoury Mince & Mash
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, british, family, comfort food, easy

Old-school savoury mince with carrots and peas in a rich gravy, spooned over creamy mash. Cheap, filling and simple.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 500g beef mince
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tbsp gravy granules
- 400ml water, boiling
- 150g frozen peas
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 25g butter
- 75ml milk
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion and carrots for 8 minutes. Add the mince and cook until browned.
2. Stir in the Worcestershire sauce, gravy granules and boiling water. Simmer for 20 minutes until the carrots are tender and the gravy has thickened. Add the peas for the last 5 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender. Drain and mash with the butter and milk.
4. Season and serve the mince over the mash.

## Notes
- Also great with Yorkshire puddings or on toast.
