# Salmon Fishcakes
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, british, family

Crispy salmon and potato fishcakes with dill and lemon, served with salad and tartare sauce.

## Ingredients
- 500g potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 2 salmon fillets
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 15g fresh dill, chopped
- 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges
- 50g plain flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- 100g panko breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 bag mixed salad leaves (about 120g)
- 4 tbsp tartare sauce
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes until tender, then drain and mash. Leave to cool a little.
2. Meanwhile, put the salmon in a pan of simmering water for 8 minutes until just cooked. Drain, then flake, removing the skin.
3. Mix the mash, salmon, spring onions, dill, lemon zest and seasoning. Shape into 8 patties and chill for 15 minutes.
4. Dust the fishcakes in the flour, dip in the egg, then coat in the panko.
5. Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and hot through.
6. Serve with the salad, tartare sauce and lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Freeze uncooked fishcakes on a tray, then bag up. Cook from frozen for a few minutes longer.
