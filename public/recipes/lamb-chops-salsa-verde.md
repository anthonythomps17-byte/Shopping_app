# Lamb Chops with Salsa Verde
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, quick

Pink, juicy lamb chops with a zingy herb salsa verde, crushed new potatoes and green beans.

## Ingredients
- 8 lamb chops
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 750g new potatoes
- 200g green beans
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the salsa verde
- 15g fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 15g fresh mint, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp capers, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 5 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender, adding the green beans for the last 4 minutes.
2. Mix all the salsa verde ingredients with a pinch of salt.
3. Rub the chops with the oil and season. Cook in a very hot griddle or frying pan for 3–4 minutes per side for pink, then rest for 5 minutes.
4. Drain the potatoes and crush lightly with a fork.
5. Serve the chops with the potatoes, beans and salsa verde spooned over.

## Notes
- The salsa verde is also great with steak, chicken or fish.
