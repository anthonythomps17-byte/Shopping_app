# Garlic Prawn Linguine
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, quick

Juicy prawns, garlic, chilli and cherry tomatoes tossed through linguine with lemon and parsley.

## Ingredients
- 350g linguine
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 300g king prawns, raw and peeled
- 15g butter
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the linguine in a large pan of salted boiling water following the pack instructions. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat. Add the garlic and chilli and cook for 1 minute without browning.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook for 3 minutes until softening. Add the prawns and cook for 2–3 minutes until pink all over.
4. Add the butter, lemon zest and juice, then toss in the linguine with a splash of the pasta water. Stir in the parsley, season and serve.

## Notes
- Add a splash of white wine with the tomatoes if you have some open.
