# Leek & Potato Soup
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: lunch, soup, british, vegetarian

A silky, comforting classic. Simple ingredients, lots of flavour.

## Ingredients
- 25g butter
- 3 leeks, sliced
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 500g potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 100ml crème fraîche
- 15g fresh chives, snipped
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the leeks and onion gently for 10 minutes until soft but not coloured. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Add the potatoes and stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until the potatoes are soft.
3. Blend until smooth, stir in the crème fraîche and season.
4. Serve topped with chives, with crusty bread.

## Notes
- Freezes well before you add the crème fraîche.
