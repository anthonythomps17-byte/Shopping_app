# Chicken Enchiladas
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, mexican, family

Tortillas stuffed with spiced chicken, peppers and black beans, baked in a smoky tomato sauce under melted cheese.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g), diced
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tin black beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 8 flour tortillas
- 120g cheddar cheese, grated
- 150ml soured cream
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped

### For the sauce
- 500g passata
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. Simmer the passata, garlic and oregano in a small pan for 10 minutes.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the onion and pepper for 6 minutes. Add the chicken and spices and cook for 6–8 minutes until the chicken is cooked through. Stir in the beans and 4 tbsp of the sauce.
4. Spread a little sauce over the base of a baking dish. Fill each tortilla with the chicken mixture, roll up and lay seam-side down in the dish.
5. Pour over the rest of the sauce and scatter with the cheese. Bake for 20 minutes until bubbling and golden.
6. Serve with soured cream and coriander.

## Notes
- Freezes well before baking.
