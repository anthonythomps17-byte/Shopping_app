# Oven-Baked Fish & Chips
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, british, family

Crunchy panko-coated cod with homemade oven chips, peas and tartare sauce, without the deep fryer.

## Ingredients
- 4 cod fillets
- 50g plain flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- 100g panko breadcrumbs
- 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), cut into chips
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 300g frozen peas
- 4 tbsp tartare sauce
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7. Toss the chips with 2 tbsp of the oil and some salt, spread out on a large baking tray and roast for 40 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp.
2. Mix the panko with the lemon zest and the rest of the oil. Season the fish, dust in the flour, dip in the egg, then press into the panko.
3. Put the fish on a lined baking tray and bake for the last 15 minutes of the chips' cooking time, until golden and cooked through.
4. Cook the peas for 3 minutes. Serve the fish and chips with the peas, tartare sauce and lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Haddock, pollock or hake work just as well as cod.
