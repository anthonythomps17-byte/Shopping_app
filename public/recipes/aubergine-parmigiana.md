# Aubergine Parmigiana
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, italian, comfort food, vegetarian

Silky slices of aubergine layered with tomato sauce, mozzarella and cheese, baked until bubbling.

## Ingredients
- 3 aubergines, sliced lengthways about 5mm thick
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 700g passata
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 15g fresh basil
- 250g mozzarella, torn
- 50g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- 50g breadcrumbs
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Brush the aubergine slices with 3 tbsp of the oil, season, and roast on baking trays for 20–25 minutes until soft and golden.
2. Meanwhile, heat the rest of the oil with the garlic for 1 minute, then add the passata and oregano and simmer for 15 minutes. Tear in the basil and season.
3. Layer the sauce, aubergine and mozzarella in a baking dish, finishing with sauce.
4. Mix the hard cheese and breadcrumbs, scatter over the top and bake for 30 minutes until golden and bubbling.
5. Rest for 10 minutes, then serve with crusty bread and a green salad.

## Notes
- Even better the next day.
