# Tuna Fishcakes
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, family, easy

Budget-friendly fishcakes made with tinned tuna and mash, coated in crispy breadcrumbs.

## Ingredients
- 600g potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 2 tins tuna (145g each), drained
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges
- 50g plain flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- 100g breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 300g frozen peas
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes until tender, then drain, mash and leave to cool a little.
2. Mix in the tuna, spring onions, sweetcorn, parsley, lemon zest and seasoning. Shape into 8 patties and chill for 15 minutes.
3. Dust in the flour, dip in the egg, then coat in the breadcrumbs.
4. Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and hot through.
5. Cook the peas and serve with the fishcakes and lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Tinned salmon works well too.
