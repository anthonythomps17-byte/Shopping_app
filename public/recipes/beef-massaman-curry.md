# Beef Massaman Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 2 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, curry, thai, slow cook

A rich, mild Thai curry with melt-in-the-mouth beef, potatoes and peanuts in a fragrant coconut sauce.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 700g braising steak, cut into large chunks
- 1 onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp massaman curry paste
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 300ml beef stock
- 500g potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 lime, juiced
- 50g roasted peanuts
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 300g jasmine rice

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large casserole and brown the beef in batches. Lift out.
2. Add the onion and curry paste and fry for 2 minutes.
3. Return the beef, pour in the coconut milk and stock, then cover and simmer gently for 1 hr 30 min.
4. Add the potatoes, fish sauce and sugar and simmer, uncovered, for 30 minutes more until the beef and potatoes are tender and the sauce has thickened.
5. Cook the rice. Stir the lime juice and peanuts into the curry and serve scattered with coriander.

## Notes
- Freezes well, but leave out the potatoes if you plan to freeze it.
