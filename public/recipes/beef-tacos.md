# Crunchy Beef Tacos
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, mexican, quick, family

Spiced beef mince in crunchy taco shells, loaded with cheese, lettuce, tomato and soured cream. A family favourite.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 500g beef mince
- 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 12 taco shells
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 100g cheddar cheese, grated
- 150ml soured cream
- 1 jar salsa (about 300g)

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the mince and cook until browned.
2. Stir in the seasoning, tomato purée and 100ml water. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick.
3. Warm the taco shells following the pack instructions.
4. Fill the shells with the beef and top with lettuce, tomato, cheese, soured cream and salsa.

## Notes
- Use soft tortillas if you prefer.
