# Chicken & Sweetcorn Soup
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, soup, quick, chinese

The takeaway favourite: a silky, savoury soup with shredded chicken, creamy sweetcorn and ribbons of egg.

## Ingredients
- 1 litre chicken stock
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tin creamed sweetcorn (418g)
- 2 cooked chicken breasts (about 300g), shredded
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Method
1. Bring the stock and ginger to a simmer. Add the sweetcorn, chicken and soy sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
2. Mix the cornflour with 3 tbsp cold water, stir into the soup and simmer for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.
3. Turn off the heat and slowly pour in the eggs in a thin stream, stirring gently, so they set into ribbons.
4. Stir in the spring onions and sesame oil, and serve.

## Notes
- Use 2 raw chicken breasts if you don't have cooked: poach them in the stock for 15 minutes first, then shred.
