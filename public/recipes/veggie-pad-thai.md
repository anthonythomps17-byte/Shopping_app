# Veggie Pad Thai
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, quick, thai, vegetarian

Rice noodles tossed with crispy tofu, egg and crunchy vegetables in a sweet, tangy sauce, topped with peanuts and lime.

## Ingredients
- 250g flat rice noodles
- 280g firm tofu, patted dry and cubed
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 150g beansprouts
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 50g roasted peanuts, roughly chopped
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

### For the sauce
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce

## Method
1. Soak or cook the noodles following the pack instructions, then drain and rinse in cold water.
2. Mix the sauce ingredients together.
3. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a wok over a high heat. Fry the tofu for 6–8 minutes until golden on all sides, then lift out.
4. Pour the eggs into the wok, scramble for 1 minute until just set, then lift out.
5. Add the rest of the oil and stir fry the pepper, carrot and garlic for 2 minutes.
6. Add the noodles, sauce, tofu, egg, beansprouts and most of the spring onions. Toss for 2 minutes until everything is hot and coated.
7. Serve topped with the peanuts, coriander, the rest of the spring onions and lime wedges.

## Notes
- Leave out the eggs to make it vegan.
- Swap the tofu for prawns or chicken if you're cooking for meat eaters.
