# Sticky BBQ Pork Ribs
Serves: 4
Time: 2 hr 45 min
Tags: dinner, bbq, slow cook

Slow-baked pork ribs in a smoky rub, finished with a sticky barbecue glaze. Serve with corn on the cob and slaw.

## Ingredients
- 1.5kg pork ribs
- 150ml barbecue sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp cider vinegar
- 4 corn on the cob
- 300g coleslaw

### For the rub
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 150°C (130°C fan), gas 2. Mix the rub and massage it all over the ribs.
2. Wrap the ribs tightly in foil and bake for 2 hours until tender.
3. Mix the barbecue sauce, honey and vinegar. Unwrap the ribs, brush generously with the glaze and turn the oven up to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7.
4. Roast for 20–25 minutes, brushing with more glaze, until sticky and charred. Meanwhile, boil the corn for 8 minutes.
5. Cut into portions and serve with the corn and coleslaw.

## Notes
- Finish them on the barbecue instead of in the oven.
