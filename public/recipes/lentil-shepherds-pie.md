# Lentil Shepherd's Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 10 min
Tags: dinner, pie, comfort food, batch cook, vegetarian

A hearty vegetarian take on the classic: lentils and vegetables in a rich gravy under cheesy mash.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely chopped
- 2 sticks celery, finely chopped
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tins green lentils (400g each), drained
- 400ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 150g frozen peas
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 25g butter
- 75ml milk
- 50g cheddar cheese, grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion, carrots and celery for 10 minutes. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes, then the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Stir in the tomato purée, then add the lentils, stock, soy sauce and thyme. Simmer for 15 minutes until thick, then stir in the peas and season.
3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes, then mash with the butter and milk.
4. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Spoon the lentils into a baking dish, top with the mash and cheese, and bake for 25 minutes until golden.

## Notes
- Freezes well. Use a vegan spread and leave out the cheese to make it vegan.
