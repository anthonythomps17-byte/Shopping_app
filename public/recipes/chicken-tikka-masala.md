# Chicken Tikka Masala
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, curry

Tender marinated chicken in a mildly spiced tomato and cream sauce. Better than a takeaway, and ready in under an hour.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless, cut into bite-sized chunks
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp sugar
- 150ml double cream
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- Salt, to taste

### For the marinade
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 1 tbsp tikka masala curry paste
- ½ lemon, juiced

### For the sauce
- 2 tbsp tikka masala curry paste

### To serve
- 300g basmati rice
- 4 naan breads

## Method
1. Mix the yoghurt, 1 tbsp curry paste and lemon juice in a bowl. Stir in the chicken, cover and chill for at least 30 minutes (or up to a day).
2. Heat a large frying pan or griddle until very hot. Shake off the excess marinade and cook the chicken in batches for 3–4 minutes per side until charred at the edges. It will finish cooking in the sauce.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion for 8–10 minutes until soft and golden, then add the garlic, ginger and 2 tbsp curry paste and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes, sugar and 100ml water. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth if you like.
5. Add the chicken and simmer for 8–10 minutes until cooked through. Stir in the cream and garam masala, season, and heat through.
6. Meanwhile cook the rice following the pack instructions and warm the naan. Scatter the curry with coriander and serve.

## Notes
- Chicken breast works too: cut it a little bigger so it stays juicy.
- For a dairy-free version, use coconut yoghurt in the marinade and coconut milk instead of cream.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months.
