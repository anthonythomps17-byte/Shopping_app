# Lamb Rogan Josh
Serves: 4
Time: 2 hr
Tags: dinner, curry, slow cook

Tender lamb slow-cooked in a rich, aromatic tomato and spice sauce. Worth the wait.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp rogan josh curry paste
- 700g diced lamb shoulder
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 300ml lamb stock
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 300g basmati rice
- 4 naan breads

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large casserole and fry the onions for 12–15 minutes until deep golden. Add the garlic, ginger and curry paste and cook for 2 minutes.
2. Add the lamb and stir until coated and lightly browned.
3. Pour in the tomatoes and stock, then cover and simmer gently for 1 hr 30 min, stirring now and then, until the lamb is tender.
4. Take off the heat and stir in the yoghurt and garam masala. Season to taste.
5. Cook the rice and warm the naan. Serve the curry scattered with coriander.

## Notes
- Diced beef works well too.
- Freezes for up to 3 months.
