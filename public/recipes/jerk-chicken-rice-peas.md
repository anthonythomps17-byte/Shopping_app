# Jerk Chicken with Rice & Peas
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, bbq, caribbean

Spicy, smoky jerk chicken with Caribbean rice and peas cooked in coconut milk.

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken thighs, skin on and bone in
- 3 tbsp jerk seasoning
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

### For the rice and peas
- 300g long grain rice
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 1 tin kidney beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- ½ tsp ground allspice

## Method
1. Mix the jerk seasoning, lime juice, soy sauce, honey and oil. Slash the chicken a few times and rub the mixture all over. Leave for at least 1 hour, or overnight in the fridge.
2. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Roast the chicken on a rack in a roasting tin for 40–45 minutes until charred and cooked through.
3. For the rice and peas, rinse the rice. Put it in a pan with the coconut milk, 300ml water, the beans, most of the spring onions, the thyme, allspice and a good pinch of salt.
4. Bring to the boil, then cover and cook on a very low heat for 20 minutes until the rice is tender. Leave covered for 5 minutes, then fluff up.
5. Serve the chicken with the rice and peas, topped with the rest of the spring onions.

## Notes
- Finish the chicken on the barbecue for extra smokiness.
- "Peas" in rice and peas means beans: kidney beans or gungo peas.
