# Classic Beef Burgers
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, bbq, quick, family

Juicy homemade burgers with melted cheese and all the trimmings. Simple and much better than shop-bought.

## Ingredients
- 500g beef mince
- 1 red onion, half finely chopped, half sliced into rings
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 egg yolk
- 4 slices cheddar cheese
- 4 brioche buns
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 4 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 750g frozen oven chips
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
2. Mix the mince, chopped onion, mustard, egg yolk and plenty of salt and pepper. Shape into 4 patties a little wider than the buns, and press a dimple in the middle of each.
3. Heat a griddle or frying pan over a high heat (or use the barbecue). Cook the burgers for 4–5 minutes per side until cooked through. Add the cheese for the last minute and cover to melt.
4. Toast the buns, then fill with lettuce, tomato, the burgers and onion rings, with ketchup and mayo.

## Notes
- Don't press the burgers while they cook, or they'll dry out.
