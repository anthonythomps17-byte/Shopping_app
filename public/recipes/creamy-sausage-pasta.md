# Creamy Sausage Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, quick, comfort food

Crumbled sausage, fennel and chilli in a creamy tomato sauce with rigatoni and spinach.

## Ingredients
- 6 pork sausages
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 150ml crème fraîche
- 350g rigatoni
- 80g baby spinach
- 30g parmesan, finely grated

## Method
1. Cook the rigatoni following the pack instructions. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Squeeze the sausage meat out of the skins. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and fry the sausage meat, breaking it up, for 6–8 minutes until golden.
3. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, then the garlic, fennel seeds and chilli for 1 minute.
4. Pour in the tomatoes and simmer for 8 minutes, then stir in the crème fraîche and spinach until wilted.
5. Toss with the pasta and a splash of the cooking water, and serve with parmesan.

## Notes
- Use spicy Italian-style sausages if you can find them.
