# Slow-Cooked Beef Ragu
Serves: 6
Time: 3 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, slow cook

Beef braised for hours in red wine and tomatoes until it falls apart, then tossed with ribbons of pappardelle.

## Ingredients
- 1kg braising steak, in large chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely chopped
- 2 sticks celery, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 250ml red wine
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 300ml beef stock
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 bay leaves
- 500g pappardelle
- 50g parmesan, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 150°C (130°C fan), gas 2. Season the beef. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof casserole and brown the beef in batches. Lift out.
2. Add the onion, carrots and celery and cook gently for 10 minutes. Add the garlic and tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the wine and bubble for 2 minutes, then add the tomatoes, stock, rosemary and bay leaves. Return the beef.
4. Cover and cook in the oven for 3 hours, until the beef falls apart. Shred it into the sauce with two forks and season.
5. Cook the pappardelle, then toss with the ragu and serve with parmesan.

## Notes
- Freezes brilliantly. Also great on polenta or mash.
