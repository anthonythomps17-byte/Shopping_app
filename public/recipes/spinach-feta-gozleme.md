# Spinach & Feta Gözleme
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, turkish, street food, vegetarian

Turkish stuffed flatbreads made with a quick no-yeast yoghurt dough, filled with spinach, feta and spring onion and cooked in a dry pan until crisp and blistered.

## Ingredients
- 300g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 200g natural yoghurt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 240g spinach
- 200g feta, crumbled
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 30g butter, melted
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mix the flour, baking powder and a pinch of salt, then add the yoghurt and oil and bring together into a soft dough. Knead for 1–2 minutes until smooth, cover and leave for 15 minutes.
2. Wilt the spinach in a pan with a splash of water, then squeeze out the water and chop. Mix with the feta, spring onions, mint, chilli and some pepper.
3. Split the dough into 4 and roll each piece out on a floured surface to a thin rectangle about 25 x 35cm.
4. Spread the filling over half of each, fold the other half over and press the edges to seal.
5. Cook one at a time in a large dry frying pan over a medium heat for 3–4 minutes per side, brushing with butter, until golden and crisp in spots.
6. Cut into strips and serve with lemon wedges to squeeze over.

## Notes
- Swap half the feta for grated mozzarella for a stretchier filling.
- Add some leftover spiced mince for a meaty version.
