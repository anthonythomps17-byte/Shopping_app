# Spicy Bean Burgers
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, bbq, family, vegetarian

Crispy-edged kidney bean and sweetcorn burgers with smoky spices, in toasted buns with avocado.

## Ingredients
- 2 tins kidney beans (400g each), drained and rinsed
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 75g breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 brioche buns
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 4 slices cheddar cheese
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mash the beans roughly with a fork, leaving some texture. Mix in the sweetcorn, onion, garlic, spices, breadcrumbs, egg and seasoning.
2. Shape into 4 burgers and chill for 15 minutes to firm up.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan over a medium heat and fry the burgers for 4–5 minutes per side until crisp. Add the cheese for the last minute.
4. Toast the buns and fill with lettuce, tomato, the burgers and avocado.

## Notes
- Use a flax "egg" and leave out the cheese to make them vegan.
