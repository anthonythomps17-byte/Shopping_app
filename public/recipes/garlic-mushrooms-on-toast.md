# Garlic Mushrooms on Toast
Serves: 2
Time: 15 min
Tags: breakfast, brunch, quick, vegetarian

Buttery garlic and thyme mushrooms with a splash of crème fraîche, piled onto crisp toast.

## Ingredients
- 25g butter
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 tbsp crème fraîche
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 4 slices sourdough
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Melt the butter in a large frying pan over a high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5–6 minutes until golden.
2. Add the garlic and thyme leaves for 1 minute, then stir in the crème fraîche and lemon juice. Season.
3. Toast the sourdough and spoon the mushrooms on top. Scatter with parsley.

## Notes
- Top with a poached or fried egg to make it more filling.
