# Tarka Dal
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, curry, batch cook, vegetarian, vegan

Comforting spiced red lentils topped with a sizzling tarka of garlic, cumin and chilli. Cheap, filling and freezable.

## Ingredients
- 250g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 300g basmati rice
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- Salt, to taste

### For the tarka
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 1 tsp garam masala

## Method
1. Put the lentils, turmeric, ginger and 1 litre water in a pan. Bring to the boil, skim off any froth, then simmer for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes more. Season.
2. Meanwhile, cook the rice following the pack instructions.
3. For the tarka, heat the oil in a frying pan and fry the onion for 10 minutes until golden. Add the garlic, cumin and mustard seeds and chilli and fry for 1–2 minutes until the seeds pop. Stir in the garam masala.
4. Stir the lemon juice into the dal, then pour the tarka over the top. Scatter with coriander and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Freezes well for up to 3 months.
