# Sweet & Sour Pork
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, chinese, fakeaway

Crispy pork with peppers and pineapple in a tangy, glossy sauce. Brighter than a takeaway, and less greasy.

## Ingredients
- 500g pork tenderloin, cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, cut into chunks
- 1 red pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 green pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 tin pineapple chunks (435g), drained, juice saved
- 300g basmati rice
- 2 spring onions, sliced

### For the sauce
- 3 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tsp cornflour

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Mix the sauce ingredients with 100ml of the saved pineapple juice.
3. Toss the pork in the cornflour with a pinch of salt. Heat the oil in a wok and fry the pork for 5–6 minutes until crisp and cooked through. Lift out.
4. Add the onion and peppers and stir fry for 3 minutes. Add the pineapple and the sauce and bubble for 2 minutes until glossy.
5. Return the pork and toss to coat. Serve on the rice with spring onions.

## Notes
- Works with chicken breast or prawns too.
