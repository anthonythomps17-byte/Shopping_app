# Creamy Ham & Pea Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, quick, family, easy

Ham, peas and pasta in a quick, creamy cheese sauce. Ready in 15 minutes from the fridge and freezer.

## Ingredients
- 350g farfalle
- 200g frozen peas
- 200g cooked ham, chopped
- 200ml crème fraîche
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 50g cheddar cheese, grated
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the pasta following the pack instructions, adding the peas for the last 2 minutes. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Return the pasta and peas to the pan over a low heat and stir in the crème fraîche, garlic, ham and cheese with a splash of the cooking water until creamy.
3. Season with black pepper and serve.

## Notes
- Add a handful of spinach or some leftover cooked broccoli.
