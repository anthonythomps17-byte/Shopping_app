# Chicken Chow Mein
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, quick, chinese, fakeaway

Stir-fried egg noodles with chicken, crunchy vegetables and a savoury soy and oyster sauce.

## Ingredients
- 250g egg noodles
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g), thinly sliced
- 1 tsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- 150g beansprouts
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated

### For the sauce
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar

## Method
1. Cook the noodles following the pack instructions, then drain and rinse in cold water.
2. Toss the chicken with the cornflour and a pinch of salt. Mix the sauce ingredients.
3. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a wok over a high heat and stir fry the chicken for 4–5 minutes until cooked through. Lift out.
4. Add the rest of the oil and stir fry the pepper and carrot for 2 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and most of the spring onions for 1 minute.
5. Add the noodles, beansprouts, chicken and sauce and toss for 2 minutes until hot. Serve topped with the rest of the spring onions.

## Notes
- Swap the chicken for prawns or tofu.
