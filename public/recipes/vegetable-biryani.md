# Vegetable Biryani
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, curry, vegetarian

Fragrant spiced rice layered with vegetables, crispy onions, cashews and fresh herbs.

## Ingredients
- 300g basmati rice
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp tikka masala curry paste
- 2 carrots, diced
- ½ cauliflower, cut into small florets
- 100g green beans, cut into short lengths
- 150g frozen peas
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 600ml vegetable stock
- 30g raisins
- 50g cashews, toasted
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 15g fresh mint, chopped

## Method
1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then soak for 15 minutes.
2. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onions for 15 minutes until deep golden and crisp. Lift out half for the topping.
3. Add the garlic, ginger and curry paste and cook for 1 minute. Add the carrots, cauliflower and beans and stir for 3 minutes, then stir in the yoghurt.
4. Drain the rice and add it to the pan with the peas, raisins and stock. Bring to the boil, then cover tightly and cook on a very low heat for 15 minutes.
5. Leave covered for 5 minutes, then fluff up. Serve topped with the crispy onions, cashews, coriander and mint.

## Notes
- Serve with extra yoghurt mixed with grated cucumber.
