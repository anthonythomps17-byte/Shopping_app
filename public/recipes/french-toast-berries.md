# French Toast with Berries
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: breakfast, brunch, family, vegetarian

Thick slices of brioche soaked in cinnamon egg and fried until golden, with berries and maple syrup.

## Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 150ml milk
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 8 slices brioche loaf
- 25g butter
- 150g raspberries
- 150g Greek yoghurt
- 4 tbsp maple syrup

## Method
1. Whisk the eggs, milk, cinnamon and sugar in a shallow dish.
2. Melt a little of the butter in a large frying pan over a medium heat.
3. Dip the brioche slices into the egg for a few seconds each side, then fry for 2 minutes per side until golden. Repeat with the rest, adding more butter as needed.
4. Serve with the raspberries, yoghurt and maple syrup.

## Notes
- Slightly stale bread works best.
