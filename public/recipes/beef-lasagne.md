# Beef Lasagne
Serves: 6
Time: 1 hr 45 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, family

Layers of rich beef ragù, creamy white sauce and pasta, baked until golden. Worth every minute.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 2 sticks celery, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 500g beef mince
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 200ml beef stock
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 250g lasagne sheets
- 125g mozzarella, torn
- 40g parmesan, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the white sauce
- 50g butter
- 50g plain flour
- 600ml milk
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and gently fry the onion, carrot and celery for 10 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute, then the mince, and cook until browned.
2. Stir in the tomato purée, tomatoes, stock and oregano. Simmer for 30 minutes until thick. Season.
3. For the white sauce, melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then whisk in the milk a little at a time. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring, until thick. Season with salt and nutmeg.
4. Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan), gas 5. Layer a third of the meat sauce, a layer of lasagne sheets and a quarter of the white sauce in a baking dish. Repeat twice, then finish with the rest of the white sauce.
5. Top with the mozzarella and parmesan and bake for 40–45 minutes until golden and bubbling. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

## Notes
- Freezes well, before or after baking.
