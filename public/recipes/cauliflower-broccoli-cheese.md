# Cauliflower & Broccoli Cheese
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, british, comfort food, vegetarian

Tender cauliflower and broccoli baked in a rich mustardy cheese sauce with a crunchy topping. Lovely with jacket potatoes.

## Ingredients
- 1 cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 head broccoli, cut into florets
- 50g butter
- 50g plain flour
- 600ml milk
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 150g cheddar cheese, grated
- 50g breadcrumbs
- 4 potatoes (for baking)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Prick the potatoes and bake for 1 hour, or microwave for 10 minutes and finish in the oven while the cheese bakes.
2. Boil the cauliflower and broccoli for 5 minutes until just tender. Drain well and put in a baking dish.
3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick. Stir in the mustard and 100g of the cheese and season.
4. Pour the sauce over the vegetables, scatter with the breadcrumbs and the rest of the cheese, and bake for 25 minutes until golden.
5. Serve with the jacket potatoes.

## Notes
- Also a great side for a Sunday roast.
