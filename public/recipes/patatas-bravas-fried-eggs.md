# Patatas Bravas with Fried Eggs
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, spanish, street food, vegetarian, family

Crispy roast potatoes with a smoky, spicy tomato sauce and a garlicky aioli, the most popular tapa in Spain. Topped with fried eggs, it's a cheap and cheerful dinner.

## Ingredients
- 1.2kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), cut into 3cm chunks
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 eggs
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the bravas sauce
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar

### For the aioli
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp lemon juice

### To serve
- 120g mixed salad leaves

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7. Toss the potatoes with 2 tbsp of the oil, the paprika and some salt. Spread out on a large tray and roast for 40–45 minutes, turning once, until crisp and golden.
2. Meanwhile, heat the rest of the oil in a pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion for 8 minutes until soft, add the garlic, paprika and chilli powder for 1 minute, then tip in the tomatoes, sugar and vinegar. Simmer for 15 minutes, then blend until smooth and season.
3. Mix the mayonnaise, garlic and lemon juice for the aioli.
4. Just before the potatoes are ready, fry the eggs in a little oil until the whites are set and the edges crisp.
5. Pile the potatoes onto plates, spoon over the bravas sauce and aioli, top each with a fried egg and scatter with parsley. Serve with the salad.

## Notes
- Leave out the eggs and serve as one of a spread of tapas.
- Add some fried chorizo if you're not cooking veggie.
