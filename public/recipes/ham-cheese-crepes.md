# Ham & Cheese Crêpes
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, brunch, french, street food

Thin French pancakes folded around ham and melting Gruyère, the way they're made at crêpe stands all over Paris. Crack an egg into the middle to make it a galette complète.

## Ingredients
- 150g plain flour
- 2 eggs
- 350ml milk
- 30g butter, melted, plus extra for the pan
- 8 slices ham
- 150g Gruyère cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
- 120g mixed salad leaves
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Whisk the flour, eggs and a pinch of salt in a bowl, then gradually whisk in the milk until smooth. Stir in the melted butter and leave to rest for 10 minutes if you have time.
2. Heat a large non-stick frying pan over a medium heat and wipe with a little butter. Pour in a small ladleful of batter and swirl to coat the base thinly. Cook for 1 minute until the edges lift, then flip and cook for 30 seconds more. Repeat to make 8 crêpes.
3. Put a crêpe back in the pan. Spread thinly with mustard, then scatter over a little cheese, add a slice of ham and a little more cheese.
4. Fold in the sides to make a square parcel and cook for 1–2 minutes until the cheese melts. Keep warm while you fill the rest.
5. Scatter with chives and serve with the salad.

## Notes
- For a galette complète, crack an egg into the middle of the crêpe in the pan, add the ham and cheese around it, and fold the edges in once the white has set.
- Sweet crêpes: leave out the salt and serve with lemon and sugar, or chocolate spread and banana.
