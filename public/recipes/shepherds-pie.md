# Shepherd's Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, pie, british, comfort food

Lamb mince and vegetables in a rich gravy, topped with buttery mash and baked until golden.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely chopped
- 500g lamb mince
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 400ml lamb stock
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 100g frozen peas
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 25g butter
- 75ml milk
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onion and carrots for 8 minutes. Add the lamb and cook until browned, spooning off any excess fat.
2. Stir in the flour and tomato purée, then add the Worcestershire sauce, stock and rosemary leaves. Simmer for 20 minutes until thick. Stir in the peas and season.
3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender. Drain and mash with the butter and milk.
4. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Spoon the lamb into a baking dish, top with the mash and fork the top into ridges.
5. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden.

## Notes
- Swap the mash for half potato, half sweet potato or swede.
