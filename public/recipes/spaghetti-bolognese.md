# Spaghetti Bolognese
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, batch cook

A rich, slow-simmered beef and tomato ragù. Make a big batch and freeze the rest.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 2 sticks celery, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 500g beef mince
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 150ml red wine, optional
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 300ml beef stock
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 bay leaves
- 350g spaghetti
- 40g parmesan, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and gently fry the onion, carrot and celery for 10 minutes until soft. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Turn up the heat, add the mince and cook, breaking it up, until browned all over.
3. Stir in the tomato purée, then the wine, if using, and bubble for 2 minutes.
4. Add the tomatoes, stock, oregano and bay leaves. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 40 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. Season.
5. Cook the spaghetti following the pack instructions, then drain.
6. Toss the spaghetti with some of the sauce, then serve topped with the rest and the parmesan.

## Notes
- Freezes for up to 3 months.
- Also makes a great base for lasagne or chilli.
