# Tuna Pasta Bake
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, family, easy

Penne, tuna and sweetcorn in a cheesy sauce, baked until golden. A cheap store-cupboard favourite.

## Ingredients
- 350g penne
- 2 tins tuna (145g each), drained
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 125g cheddar cheese, grated
- 50g breadcrumbs
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the sauce
- 40g butter
- 40g plain flour
- 600ml milk
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Cook the penne for 2 minutes less than the pack says, then drain.
2. Melt the butter in a pan, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring, until thick.
3. Take off the heat and stir in the mustard and 100g of the cheese. Season.
4. Mix the pasta, sauce, tuna, sweetcorn and spring onions, and tip into a baking dish.
5. Scatter with the breadcrumbs and the rest of the cheese, and bake for 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

## Notes
- Stir in a handful of frozen peas or some halved cherry tomatoes too.
