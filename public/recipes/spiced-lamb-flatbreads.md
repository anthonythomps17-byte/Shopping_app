# Spiced Lamb Flatbreads
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, quick, middle eastern

Crisp flatbreads topped with spiced lamb mince, then piled with salad, yoghurt and lemon. A speedy take on Turkish lahmacun.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 400g lamb mince
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 4 flatbreads
- 150g Greek yoghurt
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7.
2. Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and lamb and cook until browned, then stir in the spices and tomato purée for 2 minutes. Season.
3. Spread the lamb over the flatbreads and bake on baking trays for 6–8 minutes until crisp at the edges.
4. Top with yoghurt, tomatoes, red onion and parsley, and serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Beef mince works well too.
