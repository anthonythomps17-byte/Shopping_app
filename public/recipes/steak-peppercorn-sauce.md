# Steak with Peppercorn Sauce
Serves: 2
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, steak, classic

Pan-fried sirloin steaks with a creamy brandy and peppercorn sauce, served with chips and greens.

## Ingredients
- 2 sirloin steaks (about 250g each, 2–3cm thick)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 15g butter
- 500g frozen chips
- 100g tenderstem broccoli
- Salt, to taste

### For the sauce
- 1 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 3 tbsp brandy
- 100ml beef stock
- 100ml double cream
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

## Method
1. Take the steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
2. Pat the steaks dry and season well with salt. Heat the oil in a heavy frying pan over a high heat until just smoking.
3. Cook the steaks for 2–3 minutes per side for medium-rare, adding the butter for the last minute and spooning it over. Move to a plate to rest for 5 minutes.
4. Steam or boil the broccoli for 3–4 minutes.
5. Turn the pan down to medium. Add the shallot and cook for 2 minutes, then add the garlic and crushed peppercorns for 30 seconds.
6. Pour in the brandy and let it bubble until almost gone. Add the stock and simmer for 2 minutes, then stir in the cream, mustard and any resting juices from the steaks. Simmer until it coats the back of a spoon.
7. Slice or serve the steaks whole with the sauce, chips and broccoli.

## Notes
- Medium is about 3–4 minutes per side; well done 5–6 minutes per side for a 2cm steak.
- Swap the brandy for extra stock if you prefer.
- Ribeye or fillet work well too.
