# Chicken & Veg Stir Fry
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, quick

A quick weeknight stir fry with a sticky soy and honey sauce.

## Ingredients
- 400g chicken breast, sliced into strips
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 1 head of broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 300g egg noodles
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

### For the sauce
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp cornflour
- 1 lime, juiced

## Method
1. Cook the noodles following the pack instructions, then drain and toss with a little oil.
2. Mix the soy sauce, honey, cornflour, lime juice and 3 tbsp water in a small bowl.
3. Heat the oil in a wok over a high heat and stir fry the chicken for 5–6 minutes until golden and cooked through.
4. Add the pepper, broccoli and carrot and cook for 3 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger and most of the spring onions for 1 minute more.
5. Pour in the sauce and noodles and toss for 1–2 minutes until glossy and hot.
6. Serve topped with the rest of the spring onions.

## Notes
- Swap the chicken for prawns or tofu.
- Keeps in the fridge for 2 days.
