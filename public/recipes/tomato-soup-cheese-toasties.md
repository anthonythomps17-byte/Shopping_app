# Tomato Soup & Cheese Toasties
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: lunch, soup, family, easy, vegetarian

Silky homemade tomato soup with golden cheese toasties for dunking. A cosy, cheap lunch.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp sugar
- 500ml vegetable stock
- 100ml milk
- 8 slices bread
- 150g cheddar cheese, grated
- 25g butter, softened
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion and carrot for 8 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Add the tomatoes, purée, sugar and stock. Simmer for 15 minutes, then blend until smooth. Stir in the milk and season.
3. Meanwhile, make 4 cheese sandwiches, butter the outsides and cook in a frying pan for 3 minutes per side until golden and melted.
4. Cut the toasties into fingers and serve with the soup.

## Notes
- The soup freezes well, before you add the milk.
