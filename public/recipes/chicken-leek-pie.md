# Chicken & Leek Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, pie, british, comfort food

Tender chicken and leeks in a creamy mustard sauce under a golden puff pastry lid.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless, cut into chunks
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 15g butter
- 2 leeks, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 300ml chicken stock
- 150ml crème fraîche
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 320g puff pastry, ready rolled
- 1 egg, beaten
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. Heat the oil in a large pan and brown the chicken for 5 minutes. Lift out.
3. Add the butter and leeks and cook gently for 8 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and thyme leaves for 1 minute.
4. Stir in the flour, then gradually add the stock. Return the chicken and simmer for 10 minutes until thickened.
5. Stir in the crème fraîche and mustard, season, and spoon into a pie dish.
6. Lay the pastry over the top, trim, crimp the edges and cut a small hole in the middle. Brush with egg.
7. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the pastry is puffed and golden.

## Notes
- Serve with mash or new potatoes and green veg.
- Add a handful of frozen peas or some sliced mushrooms to the filling.
