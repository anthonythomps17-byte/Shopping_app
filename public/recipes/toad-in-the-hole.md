# Toad in the Hole
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, british, comfort food, family

Sausages baked in a big, puffy Yorkshire pudding batter. Serve with gravy and greens.

## Ingredients
- 8 pork sausages
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 140g plain flour
- 3 eggs
- 175ml milk
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 tbsp gravy granules
- 200g green beans
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7.
2. Put the sausages and oil in a roasting tin (about 20 x 30cm) and roast for 15 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, whisk the flour, eggs, milk and a pinch of salt into a smooth batter.
4. Take the tin out and, working quickly, pour the batter around the sausages. Scatter over the rosemary leaves and return to the oven for 25–30 minutes until puffed and golden. Don't open the door for the first 20 minutes.
5. Make the gravy and boil the green beans for 4 minutes. Serve straight away.

## Notes
- The oil needs to be smoking hot when the batter goes in, so it rises well.
