# Bacon & Pea Risotto
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, italian, comfort food

Creamy risotto with crispy smoked bacon, sweet peas and parmesan. Comfort in a bowl.

## Ingredients
- 200g smoked bacon lardons
- 25g butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 300g risotto rice
- 150ml white wine, optional
- 1.2 litres chicken stock, hot
- 200g frozen peas
- 50g parmesan, finely grated
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Fry the lardons in a large pan for 5 minutes until crisp. Lift out half for the topping.
2. Add half the butter and the onion and cook for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and rice and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Add the wine, if using, and stir until absorbed. Add the stock a ladleful at a time, stirring and letting each one be absorbed before adding the next, for about 20 minutes until creamy and just tender.
4. Stir in the peas for the last 3 minutes.
5. Take off the heat and stir in the parmesan, the rest of the butter and most of the mint. Serve topped with the crispy bacon, the rest of the mint and black pepper.

## Notes
- Use extra stock instead of the wine if you prefer.
