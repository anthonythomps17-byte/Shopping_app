# Macaroni Cheese
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, comfort food, family, vegetarian

Proper macaroni cheese with a rich, mustardy cheese sauce and a crunchy golden top.

## Ingredients
- 350g macaroni
- 50g butter
- 50g plain flour
- 750ml milk
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 200g cheddar cheese, grated
- 50g breadcrumbs
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Cook the macaroni for 2 minutes less than the pack says, then drain.
2. Melt the butter in a pan, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring, until thick.
3. Take off the heat and stir in the mustard and 150g of the cheese until melted. Season.
4. Mix the macaroni with the sauce and tip into a baking dish. Top with the tomato slices, breadcrumbs and the rest of the cheese.
5. Bake for 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

## Notes
- Most supermarket cheddar is vegetarian, but check the label.
