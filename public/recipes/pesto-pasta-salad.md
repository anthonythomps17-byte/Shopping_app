# Pesto Pasta Salad
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, quick, pasta, vegetarian

Pasta tossed with pesto, cherry tomatoes, mozzarella, sweetcorn and rocket. Perfect for packed lunches and picnics.

## Ingredients
- 300g fusilli
- 5 tbsp green pesto, vegetarian
- 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 125g mozzarella, torn
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 60g rocket
- 25g pine nuts, toasted
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the pasta following the pack instructions, then rinse in cold water and drain well.
2. Toss with the pesto, tomatoes, mozzarella and sweetcorn, and season.
3. Fold through the rocket and scatter with pine nuts just before serving.

## Notes
- Keeps for 2 days in the fridge. Add the rocket on the day.
