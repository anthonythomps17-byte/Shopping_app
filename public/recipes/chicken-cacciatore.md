# Chicken Cacciatore
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, italian, one pan

Italian "hunter's stew": chicken thighs slowly braised with mushrooms, tomatoes, olives and rosemary.

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken thighs, skin on and bone in
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, quartered
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 150ml red wine, optional
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 200ml chicken stock
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 75g black olives
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Season the chicken. Heat the oil in a large lidded casserole and brown the chicken skin-side down for 8 minutes, then turn and brown for 2 minutes more. Lift out.
2. Pour away all but 1 tbsp of the fat. Fry the onion and mushrooms for 8 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the wine, if using, and bubble until reduced by half. Add the tomatoes, purée, stock and rosemary.
4. Return the chicken, skin-side up, cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes. Uncover, add the olives and simmer for 10 minutes more.
5. Scatter with parsley and serve with crusty bread, pasta or mash.

## Notes
- Leave out the wine and use extra stock if you prefer.
