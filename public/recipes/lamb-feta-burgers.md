# Lamb & Feta Burgers
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, bbq, quick

Juicy lamb burgers with salty feta and fresh mint, in toasted buns with tzatziki and crunchy salad.

## Ingredients
- 500g lamb mince
- 100g feta, crumbled
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 4 brioche buns
- 200g tzatziki
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 750g frozen oven chips
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
2. Mix the lamb, feta, mint, garlic, cumin and some seasoning. Shape into 4 burgers.
3. Cook in a hot griddle pan or on the barbecue for 5–6 minutes per side until cooked through.
4. Toast the buns and fill with lettuce, tomato, the burgers, onion and a dollop of tzatziki.

## Notes
- Also good in warm pittas.
