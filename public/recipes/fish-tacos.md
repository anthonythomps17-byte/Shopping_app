# Fish Tacos with Lime Slaw
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, mexican, quick

Spiced white fish in warm tortillas with a crunchy lime slaw, avocado and soured cream.

## Ingredients
- 4 white fish fillets (cod, haddock or basa)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic granules
- 8 flour tortillas (small)
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 150ml soured cream
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped

### For the lime slaw
- ¼ red cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 carrot, grated
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tbsp mayonnaise

## Method
1. Mix all the slaw ingredients with a pinch of salt.
2. Rub the fish with the oil, spices and some salt.
3. Cook the fish in a hot frying pan for 3–4 minutes per side until it flakes easily. Break into chunks.
4. Warm the tortillas, then fill with the slaw, fish and avocado. Top with soured cream and coriander.

## Notes
- Prawns make a great swap for the fish.
