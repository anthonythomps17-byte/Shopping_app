# Zapiekanka
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, street food, vegetarian, quick, family

Poland's answer to pizza on toast: halved baguettes piled with buttery fried mushrooms and onions, baked under melted cheese and finished with a zigzag of ketchup and chives.

## Ingredients
- 2 baguettes
- 30g butter
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 500g mushrooms, finely chopped
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- 200g mild Cheddar cheese, grated
- 4 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 120g mixed salad leaves

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7.
2. Heat the butter and oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Fry the mushrooms and onion for 10–12 minutes until all the liquid has gone and they're golden. Stir in the garlic and thyme for 1 minute and season well.
3. Cut each baguette in half, then split lengthways to make 8 long pieces. Put on a baking tray, cut-side up.
4. Spread the mushrooms over the bread and cover with the cheese.
5. Bake for 8–10 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and the edges of the bread are crisp.
6. Zigzag with ketchup, scatter with chives and serve with the salad.

## Notes
- Add sliced ham under the cheese for a meaty version.
- Garlic mayo instead of ketchup is popular in Poland too.
