# Breakfast Burritos
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: breakfast, brunch, quick, vegetarian

Soft scrambled eggs with peppers, black beans and cheese, wrapped in warm tortillas with salsa and avocado.

## Ingredients
- 6 eggs
- 15g butter
- 1 red pepper, finely chopped
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tin black beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 4 flour tortillas (large)
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 jar salsa (about 300g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Melt the butter in a frying pan and cook the pepper and spring onions for 4 minutes. Add the beans to warm through.
2. Beat the eggs with some seasoning, pour into the pan and stir gently until just set.
3. Warm the tortillas, then fill with the eggs, cheese, avocado and a spoonful of salsa. Fold in the sides and roll up.

## Notes
- Wrap in foil to eat on the go.
- Add crispy bacon or chorizo if you're not cooking veggie.
