# Creamy Salmon & Pea Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, quick, family

Flakes of salmon and sweet peas in a lemony crème fraîche sauce with dill. Ready in 20 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 350g farfalle
- 2 salmon fillets
- 200g frozen peas
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 200ml crème fraîche
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 15g fresh dill, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the pasta following the pack instructions, adding the peas for the last 2 minutes. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, season the salmon and fry in the oil for 4 minutes per side until just cooked. Flake into large pieces, removing the skin.
3. Turn the heat down, add the garlic for 30 seconds, then stir in the crème fraîche, lemon zest and juice.
4. Toss in the pasta and peas with a splash of the cooking water, then gently fold in the salmon and dill. Season and serve.

## Notes
- Hot-smoked salmon works brilliantly too: just flake it in at the end.
