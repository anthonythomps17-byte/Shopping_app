# Thai Green Chicken Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, curry, thai, quick

A fragrant coconut curry with chicken, green beans and pepper. Quicker than ordering in.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp Thai green curry paste
- 500g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless, sliced
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 150ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp soft brown sugar
- 200g green beans, trimmed and halved
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 lime, juiced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped

### To serve
- 300g jasmine rice

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over a medium heat. Add the curry paste and fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken and stir to coat in the paste, then cook for 3 minutes.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and stock, then add the fish sauce and sugar. Simmer for 10 minutes.
5. Add the green beans and pepper and simmer for 5 minutes more, until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are just tender.
6. Stir in the lime juice and most of the spring onions and coriander. Taste and add more fish sauce or lime if needed.
7. Serve over the rice, topped with the rest of the spring onions and coriander.

## Notes
- Add a handful of spinach or some sliced bamboo shoots at the end.
- Use red curry paste for a deeper, smokier flavour.
