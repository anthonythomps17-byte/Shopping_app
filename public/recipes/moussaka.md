# Moussaka
Serves: 6
Time: 1 hr 45 min
Tags: dinner, greek, family, comfort food

The Greek classic: layers of golden aubergine, potato and cinnamon-spiced lamb ragù under a thick, cheesy white sauce.

## Ingredients
- 3 aubergines, sliced into 1cm rounds
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 500g potatoes, peeled and sliced 5mm thick
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 500g lamb mince
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the topping
- 50g butter
- 50g plain flour
- 600ml milk
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg
- 1 egg, beaten
- 50g parmesan, finely grated

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Brush the aubergine slices with 3 tbsp of the oil, season, and roast on baking trays for 25 minutes until soft and golden.
2. Meanwhile, boil the potato slices for 5 minutes until just tender, then drain.
3. Heat the rest of the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute, then the lamb, and cook until browned.
4. Stir in the cinnamon, oregano and tomato purée, then the tomatoes and 100ml water. Simmer for 20 minutes until thick. Stir in the parsley and season.
5. For the topping, melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick. Take off the heat, season with salt and nutmeg, and leave to cool for a few minutes before beating in the egg and half the parmesan.
6. Layer the potatoes in a large baking dish, then half the aubergines, all the lamb, then the rest of the aubergines. Pour over the topping and scatter with the rest of the parmesan.
7. Bake for 40–45 minutes until deep golden. Rest for 15 minutes before cutting.

## Notes
- Beef mince works too, and is a little cheaper.
- Make it a day ahead: it slices more neatly and tastes even better.
