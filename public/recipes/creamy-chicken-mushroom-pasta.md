# Creamy Chicken & Mushroom Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, quick

Golden chicken and mushrooms in a garlicky parmesan cream sauce with spinach stirred through. A midweek favourite.

## Ingredients
- 350g tagliatelle
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g), thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 150ml chicken stock
- 150ml double cream
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 80g baby spinach
- 40g parmesan, finely grated, plus extra to serve
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the tagliatelle in a large pan of salted boiling water following the pack instructions. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large frying pan over a high heat. Season the chicken and fry for 5–6 minutes until golden and cooked through. Lift onto a plate.
3. Add the mushrooms to the pan and cook for 5 minutes until browned, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
4. Pour in the stock and let it bubble for 2 minutes, then stir in the cream and mustard and simmer until slightly thickened.
5. Return the chicken, add the spinach and stir until wilted. Toss in the pasta and parmesan, loosening with a splash of the pasta water. Season and serve with extra parmesan.

## Notes
- Penne or fusilli work just as well.
- Use crème fraîche instead of cream for a lighter sauce.
