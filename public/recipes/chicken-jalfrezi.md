# Chicken Jalfrezi
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, curry, fakeaway

A hot, fresh curry with chicken, crunchy peppers and green chillies in a spiced tomato sauce.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, cut into chunks
- 1 red pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 green pepper, cut into chunks
- 2 green chillies, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp jalfrezi curry paste
- 600g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless, cut into chunks
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 300g basmati rice

## Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large pan over a high heat. Fry half the onions and the peppers for 4 minutes until charred at the edges but still crunchy. Lift out.
2. Add the rest of the oil and the remaining onions and cook for 8 minutes until soft. Add the chillies, garlic, ginger and curry paste for 1 minute.
3. Add the chicken and stir to coat, then pour in the tomatoes and 100ml water. Simmer for 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
4. Meanwhile, cook the rice following the pack instructions.
5. Stir the charred peppers and onions back in with the garam masala and heat through. Scatter with coriander and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Take the seeds out of the chillies for a milder curry.
