# Pork Chops with Apples & Mustard Sauce
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, quick, one pan

Juicy pork chops with caramelised apples in a creamy cider and mustard sauce.

## Ingredients
- 4 pork chops
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 15g butter
- 2 apples, cored and cut into wedges
- 1 shallot, finely chopped
- 150ml cider
- 100ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 100ml double cream
- 1kg new potatoes
- 200g green beans
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender. Add the green beans for the last 4 minutes.
2. Season the chops. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat and cook the chops for 4–5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Lift out and rest.
3. Add the butter and apples to the pan and cook for 3–4 minutes until golden. Add the shallot for 1 minute.
4. Pour in the cider and let it bubble until reduced by half. Add the stock, mustard and cream and simmer for 2 minutes until thickened.
5. Return the chops and any resting juices. Serve with the potatoes and beans.

## Notes
- Use apple juice instead of cider if you prefer.
