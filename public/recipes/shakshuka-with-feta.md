# Shakshuka with Feta
Serves: 2
Time: 30 min
Tags: brunch, dinner, quick, one pan, vegetarian

Eggs gently poached in a spiced pepper and tomato sauce, finished with salty feta. Mop it up with crusty bread.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow pepper, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 4 eggs
- 50g feta, crumbled
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 4 slices crusty bread

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan with a lid over a medium heat. Fry the onion and peppers for 10 minutes until soft and sweet.
2. Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli flakes and cook for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the tomatoes, season and simmer for 8–10 minutes until thickened.
4. Make 4 dips in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook gently for 6–8 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny.
5. Scatter over the feta and parsley and serve straight from the pan with the bread.

## Notes
- For 4 people, double everything and use a large, wide pan.
- Add a handful of spinach with the tomatoes for extra greens.
