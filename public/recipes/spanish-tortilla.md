# Spanish Tortilla
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: lunch, brunch, spanish, vegetarian

A thick, golden potato and onion omelette. Lovely warm or cold, with a crisp salad.

## Ingredients
- 150ml olive oil
- 600g potatoes (waxy, like Charlotte), thinly sliced
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 6 eggs
- 1 bag mixed salad leaves (about 120g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a 20–24cm non-stick frying pan over a medium-low heat. Add the potatoes and onion and cook gently for 20–25 minutes, turning now and then, until soft but not browned.
2. Drain the potatoes and onion in a sieve over a bowl, saving the oil.
3. Beat the eggs with plenty of salt and pepper, then stir in the potatoes and onion. Leave for 5 minutes.
4. Heat 2 tbsp of the saved oil in the pan, pour in the egg mixture and cook over a low heat for 8 minutes until almost set.
5. Put a plate over the pan, flip the tortilla onto it, then slide it back into the pan and cook for 3–4 minutes more.
6. Leave to rest for 5 minutes, then cut into wedges and serve with the salad.

## Notes
- Use the leftover oil for frying; it's full of flavour.
