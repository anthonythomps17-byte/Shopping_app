# Pesto Salmon Traybake
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, quick, traybake, easy

Salmon fillets topped with pesto, roasted on one tray with new potatoes, cherry tomatoes and green beans.

## Ingredients
- 750g new potatoes, halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 salmon fillets
- 3 tbsp green pesto
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- 200g green beans
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7. Toss the potatoes with the oil and seasoning in a large roasting tin and roast for 15 minutes.
2. Add the tomatoes and green beans to the tin and lay the salmon on top. Spread the pesto over the salmon.
3. Roast for 12–15 minutes until the salmon is just cooked and flakes easily.
4. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Works well with cod or haddock fillets too.
