# Chickpea & Feta Couscous Salad
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, easy, vegetarian

Fluffy herby couscous with chickpeas, crunchy veg, feta and lemon. Great warm or cold, and lovely in a lunchbox.

## Ingredients
- 200g couscous
- 300ml vegetable stock, hot
- 1 tin chickpeas (400g), drained
- 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
- ½ cucumber, diced
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 100g feta, crumbled
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Put the couscous in a bowl, pour over the hot stock, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff up with a fork.
2. Stir in the chickpeas, tomatoes, cucumber, onion and herbs.
3. Dress with the lemon juice and oil, season, and scatter with feta.

## Notes
- Keeps for 2 days in the fridge.
