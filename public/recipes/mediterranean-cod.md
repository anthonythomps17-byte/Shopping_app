# Mediterranean Cod with Tomatoes & Olives
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, quick, one pan

Flaky cod baked in a rich tomato sauce with olives and capers. Light, healthy and on the table in 30 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 75g black olives
- 1 tbsp capers
- 4 cod fillets
- 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges
- 15g fresh basil
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan with a lid and cook the onion for 6 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Pour in the tomatoes and simmer for 8 minutes. Stir in the olives, capers and lemon zest and season.
3. Nestle the cod into the sauce, cover and cook gently for 8–10 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
4. Scatter with basil and serve with crusty bread and lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Any firm white fish works, such as haddock, hake or pollock.
