# Chorizo & Pepper Bocadillos
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, spanish, street food, quick

Spanish baguette sandwiches stuffed with sizzling chorizo, sweet peppers and onions, Manchego and a smear of garlicky tomato.

## Ingredients
- 225g chorizo, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 red peppers, sliced
- 1 green pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
- 2 baguettes
- 2 tomatoes, halved
- 1 clove garlic, peeled
- 100g Manchego cheese, thinly sliced
- 60g rocket

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat. Fry the chorizo for 3–4 minutes until it releases its red oil, then lift out.
2. Add the peppers and onion to the pan and fry for 10–12 minutes until soft and sweet. Stir in the paprika, vinegar and chorizo for 1 minute.
3. Cut each baguette in half, then split lengthways and toast the cut sides.
4. Rub the toasted bread with the cut garlic, then with the cut tomatoes, squeezing to leave the pulp on the bread.
5. Fill with the chorizo and peppers, Manchego and rocket.

## Notes
- Mature Cheddar works if you can't find Manchego.
- Swap the chorizo for halloumi to make it veggie.
