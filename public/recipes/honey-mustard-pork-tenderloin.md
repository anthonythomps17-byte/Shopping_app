# Honey Mustard Pork Tenderloin
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, traybake, easy

Juicy roast pork fillet with a sticky honey and wholegrain mustard glaze, roasted on a tray with potatoes and carrots.

## Ingredients
- 800g pork tenderloin (2 fillets)
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 750g new potatoes, halved
- 4 carrots, cut into batons
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 200g green beans
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. Toss the potatoes, carrots and onion with the oil, thyme and seasoning in a large roasting tin. Roast for 20 minutes.
3. Mix the honey, mustard and garlic. Season the pork and brush with half the glaze.
4. Push the vegetables to the sides, put the pork in the middle and roast for 20–25 minutes, brushing with the rest of the glaze halfway, until cooked through.
5. Rest the pork for 5 minutes. Meanwhile, boil the green beans for 4 minutes. Slice the pork and serve with the vegetables.

## Notes
- Pork tenderloin is also sold as pork fillet.
