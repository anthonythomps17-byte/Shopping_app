# Creamy Mushroom & Spinach Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, quick, easy, vegetarian

Golden mushrooms, garlic and spinach in a light, creamy sauce. A fast and cheap midweek dinner.

## Ingredients
- 350g tagliatelle
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 15g butter
- 400g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 200ml crème fraîche
- 160g baby spinach
- 40g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- ½ lemon, juiced
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the pasta following the pack instructions. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil and butter in a large frying pan over a high heat and fry the mushrooms for 6–8 minutes until golden. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Turn the heat down, stir in the crème fraîche and spinach until wilted.
4. Toss in the pasta, cheese, lemon juice and a splash of the cooking water. Season and serve.

## Notes
- Use any pasta shape you have.
