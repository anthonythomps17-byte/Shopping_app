# Chorizo & Potato Hash
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: brunch, dinner, one pan

Crispy potatoes, smoky chorizo and peppers, topped with runny fried eggs. Great for brunch or a quick dinner.

## Ingredients
- 750g potatoes, cut into 2cm cubes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 150g chorizo, diced
- 1 red onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 eggs
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 6 minutes until just tender, then drain well.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and fry the chorizo for 3 minutes until it releases its oil. Lift out.
3. Add the potatoes and fry for 10 minutes, turning now and then, until golden and crisp.
4. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 5 minutes, then the garlic and paprika for 1 minute. Return the chorizo and season.
5. Make 4 hollows, crack in the eggs, cover and cook for 4–5 minutes until the whites are set. Scatter with parsley and serve.

## Notes
- Leftover boiled or roast potatoes are perfect for this.
