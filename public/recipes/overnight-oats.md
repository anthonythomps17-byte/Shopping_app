# Overnight Oats with Berries
Serves: 2
Time: 5 min, plus overnight
Tags: breakfast, quick, easy, vegetarian

Creamy oats soaked overnight in the fridge and topped with berries and honey. Five minutes the night before and breakfast is ready.

## Ingredients
- 100g porridge oats
- 200ml milk
- 150g Greek yoghurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 2 tbsp honey
- 150g blueberries
- 1 banana, sliced

## Method
1. Mix the oats, milk, yoghurt, chia seeds and half the honey in a bowl or two jars.
2. Cover and chill overnight (or for at least 4 hours).
3. In the morning, stir, loosen with a splash of milk if needed, and top with the blueberries, banana and the rest of the honey.

## Notes
- Keeps for 2 days in the fridge, so make a few at once.
- Swap the berries for grated apple and a pinch of cinnamon.
