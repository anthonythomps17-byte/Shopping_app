# Chilli con Carne
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, mexican, batch cook

A smoky, gently spiced beef and bean chilli. Even better the next day.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 500g beef mince
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin kidney beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 250ml beef stock
- 300g long grain rice
- 150ml soured cream
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onion and pepper for 6–8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Add the mince and cook until browned, then stir in the spices and tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, beans and stock. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 30–40 minutes until thick. Season.
4. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
5. Serve the chilli on the rice with soured cream and coriander.

## Notes
- Freezes for up to 3 months.
- Great in jacket potatoes or with tortilla chips.
