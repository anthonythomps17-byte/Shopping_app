# Mushroom Stroganoff
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, quick, vegetarian

Meaty mushrooms in a creamy, smoky paprika sauce. A comforting dinner that's on the table in 25 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 15g butter
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 600g chestnut mushrooms, thickly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 200ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 150ml soured cream
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 300g basmati rice

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Heat the oil and butter in a large frying pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion for 6–8 minutes until soft.
3. Turn up the heat, add the mushrooms and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring now and then, until golden and any liquid has gone.
4. Add the garlic and paprika and cook for 1 minute, then pour in the stock, soy sauce and mustard. Bubble for 3 minutes.
5. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the soured cream. Season, scatter with parsley and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Don't let the sauce boil once the soured cream is in, or it may split.
- Also good with tagliatelle or buttery mashed potato.
