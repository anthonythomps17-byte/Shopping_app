# Crispy Calamari with Aioli
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, lunch, spanish, street food, fakeaway

Tender squid rings in a light, crunchy coating, fried in minutes and served Spanish-style with garlicky aioli, lemon and crusty bread.

## Ingredients
- 500g squid rings
- 75g plain flour
- 50g cornflour
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 litre vegetable oil, for frying
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 1 baguette
- 120g mixed salad leaves
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the aioli
- 5 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp lemon juice

## Method
1. Mix together the aioli ingredients and chill until needed.
2. Pat the squid very dry with kitchen paper. Mix the flour, cornflour, paprika, 1 tsp salt and some pepper in a bowl.
3. Heat the oil in a large, deep pan to 180°C, or until a cube of bread browns in 30 seconds. Never fill the pan more than a third full.
4. Toss a handful of squid in the flour, shake off the excess and fry for 1–2 minutes until lightly golden. Lift out onto kitchen paper and sprinkle with salt. Repeat with the rest.
5. Scatter with parsley and serve straight away with the aioli, lemon wedges, sliced baguette and salad.

## Notes
- Frozen squid rings are cheap and work perfectly: defrost and dry them well.
- Overcooking makes squid chewy, so keep the frying short.
