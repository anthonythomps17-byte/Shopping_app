# Bacon & Egg Breakfast Muffins
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: breakfast, quick, family

Toasted English muffins stacked with crispy bacon, a fried egg and melted cheese. Better than the drive-through.

## Ingredients
- 4 English muffins
- 8 rashers smoked back bacon
- 4 eggs
- 4 slices cheddar cheese
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 15g butter
- 4 tbsp tomato ketchup

## Method
1. Grill the bacon for 3–4 minutes per side until crisp.
2. Meanwhile, fry the eggs in the oil. Use egg rings, if you have them, so they fit the muffins.
3. Split and toast the muffins, then butter them.
4. Fill each muffin with bacon, an egg and a slice of cheese, with ketchup or brown sauce.

## Notes
- Swap the bacon for a sausage patty: squeeze the meat out of a pork sausage and flatten it.
