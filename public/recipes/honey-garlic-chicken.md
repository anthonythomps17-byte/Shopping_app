# Honey Garlic Chicken
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, quick, fakeaway

Sticky, glossy chicken thighs in a sweet and savoury honey, soy and garlic glaze, with rice and greens.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 300g basmati rice
- 200g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

### For the sauce
- 4 tbsp honey
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp cornflour

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Mix the honey, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, vinegar and cornflour with 3 tbsp water.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Season the chicken and cook for 5–6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
4. Pour in the sauce and let it bubble for 2–3 minutes, turning the chicken, until thick and sticky.
5. Meanwhile, steam or boil the broccoli for 3–4 minutes.
6. Serve the chicken on the rice with the broccoli, topped with spring onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Works with chicken breast or salmon fillets too.
