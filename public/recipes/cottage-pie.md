# Cottage Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, pie, british, comfort food

Savoury beef mince and vegetables under a fluffy, cheesy mash topping. A proper family favourite.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely chopped
- 500g beef mince
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 400ml beef stock
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 100g frozen peas
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 25g butter
- 75ml milk
- 50g cheddar cheese, grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onion and carrots for 8 minutes. Add the mince and cook until browned.
2. Stir in the flour and tomato purée, then add the Worcestershire sauce, stock and thyme. Simmer for 20 minutes until thick, then stir in the peas and season.
3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender. Drain and mash with the butter and milk. Season.
4. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Spoon the mince into a baking dish, top with the mash and rough it up with a fork. Scatter with the cheese.
5. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and bubbling.

## Notes
- Make it with lamb mince and it becomes shepherd's pie.
- Freezes well, before or after baking.
