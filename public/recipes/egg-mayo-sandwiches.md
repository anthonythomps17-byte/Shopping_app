# Egg Mayo Sandwiches
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, easy, vegetarian

The classic: creamy chopped egg mayonnaise with chives and crunchy lettuce. Cheap, quick and perfect for lunchboxes.

## Ingredients
- 6 eggs
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 15g fresh chives, snipped
- 8 slices bread
- 15g butter, softened
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
2. Roughly mash the eggs with the mayonnaise, mustard, chives and some seasoning.
3. Butter the bread, fill with the egg mayo and lettuce, and cut into quarters.

## Notes
- Keep the filling in the fridge for up to 2 days.
