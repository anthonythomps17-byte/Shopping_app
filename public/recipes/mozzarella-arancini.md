# Mozzarella Arancini
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, italian, street food, vegetarian

Sicilian risotto balls with a molten mozzarella middle, crumbed and fried until golden, served with a quick tomato sauce for dipping. A great use for leftover risotto.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 300g risotto rice
- 1 litre vegetable stock, hot
- 50g vegetarian hard cheese, grated
- 25g butter
- 125g mozzarella, cut into 16 cubes
- 50g plain flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 100g breadcrumbs
- 1 litre vegetable oil, for frying
- 120g mixed salad leaves
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the tomato sauce
- 500g passata
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 15g fresh basil

## Method
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over a medium heat and fry the onion for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and rice and stir for 1 minute.
2. Add the stock a ladleful at a time, stirring and letting each one be absorbed before adding the next, for about 20 minutes until the rice is tender. Stir in the cheese and butter, season, then spread out on a tray to cool completely.
3. Simmer the passata, garlic and oregano for 10 minutes. Tear in the basil and season.
4. With damp hands, take a golf ball of risotto, flatten it in your palm, put a mozzarella cube in the middle and shape the rice around it into a ball. Repeat to make 16.
5. Roll each ball in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
6. Heat the oil in a large, deep pan to 180°C, or until a cube of bread browns in 30 seconds. Never fill the pan more than a third full. Fry the arancini in batches for 4–5 minutes until deep golden. Drain on kitchen paper.
7. Serve hot with the tomato sauce and the salad.

## Notes
- To bake instead, spray with oil and bake at 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7 for 20–25 minutes, turning once.
- Make the risotto the day before; it's easier to shape when cold.
