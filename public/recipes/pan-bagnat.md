# Pan Bagnat
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min, plus pressing
Tags: lunch, french, street food

The famous pressed sandwich from Nice: a niçoise salad of tuna, egg, tomato, peppers and olives packed into crusty bread and left to soak up the dressing. Perfect for picnics.

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 2 tins tuna in olive oil (145g each), drained
- 1 baguette
- 3 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 green pepper, thinly sliced
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 50g pitted black olives, sliced
- 15g fresh basil
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the dressing
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 clove garlic, crushed

## Method
1. Boil the eggs for 8 minutes, then cool in cold water, peel and slice.
2. Whisk together the dressing ingredients with some salt and pepper.
3. Cut the baguette into 4, then split each piece lengthways. Pull out a little of the soft middle and drizzle the cut sides with half the dressing.
4. Layer the lettuce, tomatoes, tuna, egg, pepper, spring onions, olives and basil on the bottom halves, drizzle with the rest of the dressing and put the tops on.
5. Wrap each tightly in baking paper or foil, then put a chopping board and a few tins on top. Leave for at least 30 minutes, or up to a few hours in the fridge, before eating.

## Notes
- Traditionally it has a few anchovies too.
- Round crusty rolls work just as well as a baguette.
