# Spinach & Feta Spanakopita
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 10 min
Tags: dinner, lunch, greek, street food, vegetarian

A big golden filo pie filled with spinach, feta, spring onions and dill, sold by the slice in every Greek bakery. Lovely warm or cold the next day.

## Ingredients
- 500g spinach
- 200g feta, crumbled
- 125g ricotta
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 6 spring onions, sliced
- 15g fresh dill, chopped
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, zested
- ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
- 270g filo pastry
- 60g butter, melted
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- 3 tomatoes, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan), gas 5.
2. Wilt the spinach in a large pan with a splash of water, in batches if you need to. Tip into a colander, cool a little, then squeeze out as much water as you can and chop.
3. Mix the spinach with the feta, ricotta, eggs, spring onions, herbs, lemon zest, nutmeg and some pepper.
4. Brush a roughly 20 x 30cm baking dish with butter. Lay in half the filo sheets, brushing each with butter and letting them hang over the sides.
5. Spread in the filling, fold the overhanging edges over, then top with the rest of the filo, brushing each sheet with butter and scrunching the top ones a little.
6. Score the top into squares, scatter with the sesame seeds and bake for 40–45 minutes until deep golden and crisp.
7. Rest for 10 minutes, then cut into squares and serve with the cucumber and tomatoes.

## Notes
- Frozen spinach works: use 600g, defrosted and squeezed very dry.
- Cut into squares and freeze for lunchboxes.
