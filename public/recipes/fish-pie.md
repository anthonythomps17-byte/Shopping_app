# Fish Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, pie, british, comfort food

Chunks of salmon, white fish and smoked haddock in a creamy parsley sauce under buttery mash.

## Ingredients
- 400g fish pie mix
- 150g king prawns, raw and peeled
- 500ml milk
- 1 bay leaf
- 50g butter
- 40g plain flour
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 100g frozen peas
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 50g cheddar cheese, grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Put the fish and bay leaf in a pan with the milk, bring to a gentle simmer and poach for 5 minutes. Lift the fish into a baking dish and strain the milk into a jug.
2. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender. Drain and mash with 15g of the butter and a splash of the poaching milk.
3. Melt the rest of the butter in a pan, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the poaching milk. Simmer for 3 minutes until thick. Add the parsley and lemon juice and season.
4. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Scatter the prawns and peas over the fish, pour over the sauce, then top with the mash and cheese.
5. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and bubbling.

## Notes
- Fish pie mix is in the fish aisle. Any mix of white fish, salmon and smoked fish works.
