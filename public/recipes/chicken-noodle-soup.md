# Chicken Noodle Soup
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: lunch, soup, comfort food

A light, comforting soup with tender chicken, vegetables and noodles. Just what you want when you're under the weather.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 sticks celery, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1.5 litres chicken stock
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g)
- 150g egg noodles
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- ½ lemon, juiced
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and gently fry the onion, carrots and celery for 8 minutes until softening. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Pour in the stock and bring to a simmer. Add the whole chicken breasts and poach for 15 minutes until cooked through.
3. Lift out the chicken and shred it with two forks.
4. Add the noodles to the pan and cook for 4 minutes, then return the chicken.
5. Stir in the parsley and lemon juice, season and serve.

## Notes
- Keeps for 3 days in the fridge, but the noodles soften, so add them fresh when reheating if you can.
