# Chicken Shish Kebabs
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, turkish, street food, bbq, family

Turkish tavuk şiş: chicken marinated in yoghurt, tomato and spices, grilled on skewers and served with buttery orzo rice, a sharp onion salad and garlic yoghurt.

## Ingredients
- 700g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless, cut into chunks
- 100g natural yoghurt
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 red peppers, cut into chunks
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the rice
- 25g butter
- 75g orzo
- 250g basmati rice
- 500ml chicken stock

### For the salad and yoghurt
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ground sumac
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 3 tomatoes, chopped
- ½ cucumber, chopped
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 1 clove garlic, crushed

## Method
1. Mix the yoghurt, tomato purée, oil, garlic, spices, half the lemon juice and plenty of salt and pepper. Stir in the chicken and leave for at least 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.
2. Thread the chicken and peppers onto 8 skewers (soak wooden ones in water first).
3. For the rice, melt the butter in a pan over a medium heat and fry the orzo for 2–3 minutes until golden. Stir in the rice, add the stock and a pinch of salt, bring to the boil, then cover and cook on the lowest heat for 12 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes.
4. Heat the grill to high, or use a barbecue or griddle pan. Cook the kebabs for 12–15 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through.
5. Toss the onion with the sumac, parsley and the rest of the lemon juice, then mix with the tomatoes and cucumber. Mix the yoghurt with the garlic and a pinch of salt.
6. Serve the kebabs on the rice with the salad and garlic yoghurt.

## Notes
- Wrap in flatbreads instead of serving with rice for a takeaway-style kebab.
