# Banana & Honey Porridge
Serves: 2
Time: 10 min
Tags: breakfast, quick, easy, vegetarian

Creamy porridge with sliced banana, honey and a pinch of cinnamon. Warming, cheap and ready in 10 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 100g porridge oats
- 300ml milk
- 1 banana, sliced
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon
- 100g blueberries

## Method
1. Put the oats, milk and 300ml water in a pan with a pinch of salt.
2. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 4–5 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
3. Spoon into bowls and top with the banana, blueberries, honey and cinnamon.

## Notes
- Microwave it instead: cook in a large bowl for 2 minutes, stir, then 1–2 minutes more.
