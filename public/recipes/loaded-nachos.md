# Loaded Nachos
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, quick, mexican, family, vegetarian

A big tray of tortilla chips piled with spiced beans, melted cheese, salsa, guacamole and soured cream. Made for sharing.

## Ingredients
- 200g tortilla chips
- 1 tin kidney beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 150g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 jar salsa (about 300g)
- 150g guacamole
- 150ml soured cream
- 2 tbsp jalapeños, sliced
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. Roughly mash the beans with the spices and a splash of water.
3. Spread half the tortilla chips over a large ovenproof dish, dot with half the beans and cheese, then repeat.
4. Bake for 8–10 minutes until the cheese has melted.
5. Top with salsa, guacamole, soured cream, jalapeños, spring onions and coriander, and serve straight away.

## Notes
- Add leftover chilli or pulled pork instead of the beans.
