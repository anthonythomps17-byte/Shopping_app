# Stuffed Peppers with Rice & Feta
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, easy, vegetarian

Sweet roasted peppers filled with herby tomato rice and feta, baked until golden.

## Ingredients
- 4 red peppers, halved and deseeded
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 250g microwave rice
- 250g cherry tomatoes, quartered
- 100g feta, crumbled
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 bag mixed salad leaves (about 120g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Put the peppers cut-side up in a roasting tin, brush with half the oil and roast for 15 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, heat the rest of the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion for 6 minutes. Add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the rice and tomatoes and heat through. Take off the heat and fold in most of the feta and the parsley. Season.
4. Spoon the rice into the peppers, top with the rest of the feta and bake for 15–20 minutes until golden.
5. Serve with the salad.

## Notes
- Swap the feta for grated cheddar or goat's cheese.
