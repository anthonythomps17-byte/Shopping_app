# Lángos
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 30 min, including proving
Tags: lunch, street food, vegetarian, treat

Hungary's favourite street snack: puffy fried bread brushed with garlic, then spread with soured cream and showered with grated cheese. Big, messy and very moreish.

## Ingredients
- 500g strong white bread flour
- 7g fast-action dried yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 200ml milk, warm
- 150ml water, warm
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil, plus 1 litre for frying
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 300ml soured cream
- 200g mild Cheddar cheese, grated
- 4 spring onions, sliced

### To serve
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 3 tomatoes, sliced

## Method
1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt in a bowl. Add the milk, water and 2 tbsp vegetable oil and bring together into a soft dough. Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth, then cover and leave somewhere warm for 1 hour, until doubled in size.
2. Mix the garlic with the olive oil and a pinch of salt.
3. Split the dough into 8. With oiled hands, stretch each piece into a round about 15cm across, thinner in the middle.
4. Heat the oil for frying in a large, deep pan to 180°C, or until a cube of bread browns in 30 seconds. Never fill the pan more than a third full. Fry one lángos at a time for 1–2 minutes per side until puffed and golden. Drain on kitchen paper.
5. Brush each hot lángos with the garlic oil, spread with soured cream and pile on the cheese and spring onions. Serve with the cucumber and tomatoes.

## Notes
- Add some crispy bacon or ham on top for meat eaters.
