# Halloumi Pittas
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, vegetarian

Golden griddled halloumi stuffed into warm pittas with hummus, crunchy salad and sweet chilli sauce.

## Ingredients
- 225g halloumi, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 pittas
- 200g hummus
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- ½ lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Heat a griddle or frying pan over a medium-high heat. Brush the halloumi with oil and cook for 2 minutes per side until golden. Squeeze over the lemon.
2. Warm the pittas and split them open.
3. Spread with hummus, then fill with lettuce, tomato, cucumber and the halloumi. Drizzle with sweet chilli sauce.

## Notes
- Add a few slices of roasted pepper from a jar.
