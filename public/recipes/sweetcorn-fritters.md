# Sweetcorn Fritters
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: brunch, lunch, quick, easy, vegetarian

Crispy sweetcorn and spring onion fritters with salsa, avocado and soured cream. Made mostly from the cupboard.

## Ingredients
- 2 tins sweetcorn (198g each), drained
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 150g self-raising flour
- 2 eggs
- 150ml milk
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 jar salsa (about 300g)
- 150ml soured cream
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk and paprika into a thick batter. Stir in the sweetcorn and spring onions and season.
2. Heat a little oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat. Add heaped tablespoons of the batter and fry for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Keep warm while you cook the rest.
3. Serve with avocado, salsa and soured cream.

## Notes
- Add a fried egg on top for a bigger brunch.
