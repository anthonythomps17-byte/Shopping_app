# Prawn Paella
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, spanish, one pan

Saffron rice with prawns, peppers and peas, cooked in one big pan and served with lemon.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 pinch saffron
- 300g paella rice
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 300g king prawns, raw and peeled
- 150g frozen peas
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large, wide frying pan or paella pan. Fry the onion and pepper for 6–8 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and paprika for 1 minute.
2. Crumble the saffron into the stock. Stir the rice into the pan to coat it in the oil, then add the tomatoes and stock.
3. Simmer for 18–20 minutes without stirring, until the rice is almost tender and most of the liquid has gone.
4. Push the prawns into the rice and scatter over the peas. Cook for 5 minutes more until the prawns are pink.
5. Season, scatter with parsley and serve from the pan with lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Don't stir once the stock is in. A crispy layer on the bottom is the best bit.
