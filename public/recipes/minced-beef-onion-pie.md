# Minced Beef & Onion Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 10 min
Tags: dinner, pie, british, family, comfort food

Rich minced beef and onion gravy under a flaky puff pastry lid. Simple, cheap and very comforting.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, chopped
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 500g beef mince
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 400ml beef stock
- 320g puff pastry, ready rolled
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 25g butter
- 200g frozen peas
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onions and carrot for 10 minutes until soft. Add the mince and cook until browned.
2. Stir in the flour and tomato purée, then add the Worcestershire sauce and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes until thick. Season and cool a little.
3. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Spoon the filling into a pie dish, cover with the pastry, crimp the edges and brush with egg.
4. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden.
5. Meanwhile, boil and mash the potatoes with the butter, and cook the peas. Serve with the pie.

## Notes
- Make the filling the day before to save time.
