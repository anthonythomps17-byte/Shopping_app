# Vegetable & Cheese Frittata
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, brunch, quick, easy, vegetarian

A thick, golden oven-finished omelette with potatoes, peppers, peas and cheese. Great for using up odds and ends.

## Ingredients
- 400g new potatoes, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 100g frozen peas
- 8 eggs
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 bag mixed salad leaves (about 120g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 8 minutes until tender, then drain. Heat the grill to high.
2. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof frying pan and cook the pepper and spring onions for 4 minutes. Add the potatoes and peas.
3. Beat the eggs with half the cheese and plenty of seasoning, pour into the pan and cook over a low heat for 8 minutes until almost set.
4. Scatter with the rest of the cheese and grill for 3–4 minutes until puffed and golden.
5. Cut into wedges and serve with the salad.

## Notes
- Good cold the next day, too.
