# Sausage & Veg Traybake
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, traybake, family, easy

Sausages, potatoes, peppers and red onions roasted together in one tin. Hardly any washing up.

## Ingredients
- 8 pork sausages
- 750g new potatoes, halved
- 2 red peppers, cut into chunks
- 2 red onions, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. Toss the potatoes, peppers and onions with the oil, rosemary, paprika and seasoning in a large roasting tin. Roast for 15 minutes.
3. Add the sausages and roast for 20 minutes, then add the tomatoes and roast for 10 minutes more, until the sausages are browned and cooked through.

## Notes
- Add a drizzle of honey and mustard to the sausages for the last 10 minutes.
