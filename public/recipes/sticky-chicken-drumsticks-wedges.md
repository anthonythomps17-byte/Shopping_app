# Sticky Chicken Drumsticks & Wedges
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, family, easy, traybake

Sweet and sticky honey-soy drumsticks roasted on the same tray as crispy paprika potato wedges. Kids love it.

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken drumsticks
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1kg potatoes, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 corn on the cob
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. Mix the honey, soy sauce, ketchup and garlic, and toss the drumsticks in it.
3. Toss the wedges with the oil, paprika and some salt, and spread out in a large roasting tin. Roast for 10 minutes.
4. Add the drumsticks to the tin and roast for 35–40 minutes, turning once and spooning over any sauce, until sticky and cooked through.
5. Boil the corn for 8 minutes and serve with the chicken and wedges.

## Notes
- Thighs work just as well as drumsticks.
