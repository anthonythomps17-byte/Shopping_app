# Prawn Cocktail Salad
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, british

A retro favourite: juicy prawns in a tangy Marie Rose sauce with crisp lettuce, avocado and buttered brown bread.

## Ingredients
- 300g cooked king prawns, peeled
- 2 little gem lettuces, shredded
- 1 avocado, diced
- ½ cucumber, diced
- 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 4 slices brown bread
- 15g butter
- 1 pinch smoked paprika

### For the Marie Rose sauce
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- ½ lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Mix the sauce ingredients together.
2. Toss the prawns with half the sauce.
3. Divide the lettuce, avocado, cucumber and tomatoes between plates or bowls. Top with the prawns and the rest of the sauce, and sprinkle with paprika.
4. Serve with lemon wedges and buttered brown bread.

## Notes
- Frozen cooked prawns are cheaper: defrost them in the fridge overnight.
