# Croque Monsieur
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, quick, french

The French café classic: a ham and cheese toastie topped with bubbling, cheesy white sauce.

## Ingredients
- 8 slices bread
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 125g cooked ham
- 150g gruyère cheese, grated
- 15g butter, softened

### For the sauce
- 15g butter
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 150ml milk
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg

## Method
1. Heat the grill to high. For the sauce, melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then whisk in the milk. Simmer for 2 minutes until thick, then season with nutmeg and stir in a third of the cheese.
2. Spread 4 slices of bread with mustard, top with the ham and half the remaining cheese, and close with the other slices.
3. Butter the outsides and toast in a frying pan for 2 minutes per side until golden.
4. Put on a baking tray, spread the sauce on top, scatter with the rest of the cheese and grill for 3–4 minutes until bubbling.

## Notes
- Top with a fried egg and it becomes a croque madame.
- Cheddar works if you don't have gruyère.
