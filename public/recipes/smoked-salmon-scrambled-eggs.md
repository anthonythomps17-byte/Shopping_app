# Smoked Salmon & Scrambled Eggs
Serves: 2
Time: 10 min
Tags: brunch, quick

Soft, buttery scrambled eggs with smoked salmon and chives on toasted sourdough. A special breakfast in 10 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 15g butter
- 2 tbsp milk
- 100g smoked salmon
- 15g fresh chives, snipped
- 2 slices sourdough
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Beat the eggs with the milk and some black pepper.
2. Toast the sourdough.
3. Melt the butter in a non-stick pan over a low heat. Add the eggs and stir slowly and gently for 3–4 minutes, until just set and still creamy. Take off the heat.
4. Pile the eggs onto the toast, top with the smoked salmon and chives, and serve straight away.

## Notes
- Take the eggs off the heat a moment before they look done: they keep cooking.
