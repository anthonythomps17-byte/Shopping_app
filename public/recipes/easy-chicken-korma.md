# Easy Chicken Korma
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, curry, family, easy

A mild, creamy, slightly sweet curry that's perfect for kids and anyone who doesn't like it hot.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp korma curry paste
- 600g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless, cut into chunks
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 30g ground almonds
- 1 tsp sugar
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 300g basmati rice
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, ginger and curry paste and cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the chicken and stir to coat, then pour in the coconut milk and add the ground almonds and sugar. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
3. Meanwhile, cook the rice following the pack instructions.
4. Take the curry off the heat and stir in the yoghurt. Scatter with coriander and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Add a handful of frozen peas or spinach for extra veg.
