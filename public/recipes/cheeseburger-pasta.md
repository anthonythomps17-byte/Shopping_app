# Cheeseburger Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, family, quick, easy

Everything you love about a cheeseburger in a one-pan pasta: beef, tomato, mustard and lots of melted cheese.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 500g beef mince
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 300g macaroni
- 750ml beef stock
- 100ml milk
- 150g cheddar cheese, grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large, deep frying pan and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the mince and cook until browned, then the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Stir in the tomato purée, ketchup and mustard, then add the macaroni and stock.
3. Bring to the boil, then simmer, stirring often, for 12–14 minutes until the pasta is tender and most of the liquid has gone.
4. Stir in the milk and cheese until melted and creamy. Season and serve.

## Notes
- Top with chopped gherkins or a few slices of tomato, burger-style.
