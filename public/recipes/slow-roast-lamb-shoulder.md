# Slow-Roast Lamb Shoulder
Serves: 6
Time: 4 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, sunday roast, british

Lamb shoulder roasted low and slow with garlic and rosemary until it falls off the bone. Serve with roast potatoes, carrots and gravy.

## Ingredients
- 2kg lamb shoulder, bone in
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced
- 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 onions, thickly sliced
- 300ml lamb stock
- 1.2kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and cut into chunks
- 4 tbsp vegetable oil
- 500g carrots, cut into batons
- 300g frozen peas
- 2 tbsp gravy granules
- 4 tbsp mint sauce
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 160°C (140°C fan), gas 3. Make small cuts all over the lamb and push in the garlic slices and small sprigs of rosemary. Season well.
2. Put the onions in a roasting tin, sit the lamb on top and pour the stock around it. Cover tightly with foil and roast for 4 hours.
3. With 1 hour to go, boil the potatoes for 8 minutes, drain and rough up. Roast in a separate tin with the hot oil for 50–60 minutes, turning halfway. Turn the oven up to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6 when the potatoes go in, and take the foil off the lamb.
4. Rest the lamb for 20 minutes. Boil the carrots for 8 minutes and the peas for 3 minutes.
5. Make the gravy with the granules and the juices from the lamb tin. Pull the lamb apart and serve with the vegetables, gravy and mint sauce.

## Notes
- Leftovers make brilliant wraps with tzatziki.
