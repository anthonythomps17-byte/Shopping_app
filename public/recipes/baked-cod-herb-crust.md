# Baked Cod with a Herb Crust
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, quick, easy

Flaky cod under a crisp lemon and herb crust, with crushed new potatoes and green beans.

## Ingredients
- 4 cod fillets
- 50g breadcrumbs
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 25g butter, melted
- 750g new potatoes
- 200g green beans
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender.
2. Mix the breadcrumbs, parsley, lemon zest, garlic and melted butter with some seasoning.
3. Put the cod on a lined baking tray, season, and press the crumb mixture on top. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
4. Add the green beans to the potatoes for the last 4 minutes, then drain.
5. Lightly crush the potatoes with the oil, lemon juice and seasoning. Serve with the cod and beans.

## Notes
- Works with salmon, haddock or hake too.
