# Cheesy Chicken & Broccoli Pasta Bake
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, family, easy

Chicken, broccoli and pasta in a creamy cheese sauce, baked until bubbling. A crowd-pleaser that's easy on the wallet.

## Ingredients
- 300g penne
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g), diced
- 150g cheddar cheese, grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the sauce
- 40g butter
- 40g plain flour
- 600ml milk
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Cook the penne for 2 minutes less than the pack says, adding the broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the chicken for 6–8 minutes until cooked through.
3. Melt the butter in a pan, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick. Stir in the mustard and 100g of the cheese and season.
4. Mix the pasta, broccoli, chicken and sauce, tip into a baking dish and top with the rest of the cheese.
5. Bake for 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

## Notes
- Leftover roast chicken works perfectly here.
