# BLT Sandwich
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, british

Crispy bacon, crunchy lettuce and juicy tomato on toasted bread with plenty of mayo. A simple classic done right.

## Ingredients
- 12 rashers smoked back bacon
- 8 slices bread
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- 3 tomatoes, sliced
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Grill the bacon for 3–4 minutes per side until crisp.
2. Toast the bread and spread with mayonnaise.
3. Layer the lettuce, tomato and bacon on 4 slices, season with black pepper and top with the other slices. Cut in half to serve.

## Notes
- Add sliced avocado for a BLAT.
