# Chicken Katsu Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, japanese, fakeaway

Crunchy panko-coated chicken with a mild, sweet Japanese-style curry sauce and fluffy rice.

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts (about 600g)
- 50g plain flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 100g panko breadcrumbs
- 4 tbsp vegetable oil
- 300g basmati rice
- 2 spring onions, sliced

### For the curry sauce
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp medium curry powder
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 500ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp soy sauce

## Method
1. For the sauce, heat the oil in a pan and fry the onion and carrots for 8 minutes until softened. Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and flour and cook for 1 minute.
2. Gradually stir in the stock, then add the honey and soy sauce. Simmer for 15 minutes, then blend until smooth.
3. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Bash the chicken breasts between cling film until about 1.5cm thick.
4. Dip each breast in the flour, then the egg, then the panko. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and fry for 2 minutes per side until golden.
5. Move to a baking tray and bake for 12–15 minutes until cooked through. Meanwhile, cook the rice.
6. Slice the chicken and serve on the rice with the curry sauce poured alongside, topped with spring onions.

## Notes
- The sauce freezes well, so make a double batch.
