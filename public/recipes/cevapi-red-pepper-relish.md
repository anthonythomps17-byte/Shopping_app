# Ćevapi with Red Pepper Relish
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, street food, bbq, family

Little garlicky skinless sausages from the Balkans, grilled and stuffed into warm flatbreads with raw onion, soured cream and a smoky roasted red pepper relish.

## Ingredients
- 750g beef mince (15–20% fat)
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 4 flatbreads
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 150ml soured cream

### For the red pepper relish
- 1 jar roasted red peppers (460g), drained
- 1 clove garlic, peeled
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp olive oil

### To serve
- 1 cucumber, chopped
- 3 tomatoes, chopped

## Method
1. Put the mince, garlic, paprika, bicarbonate of soda, salt and pepper in a bowl and squish together with your hands for 3–4 minutes until sticky. Chill for 15 minutes if you have time.
2. Blend the relish ingredients until fairly smooth and season.
3. With wet hands, roll the meat into about 24 finger-shaped sausages, roughly 8cm long.
4. Heat a griddle pan or barbecue until very hot. Cook the ćevapi for 8–10 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through.
5. Warm the flatbreads on the griddle. Fill each with ćevapi, onion, a spoonful of soured cream and the pepper relish. Serve with the cucumber and tomatoes.

## Notes
- The bicarbonate of soda keeps them light and springy, so don't leave it out.
- Shop-bought ajvar can be used instead of making the relish.
