# Sausage & Mash with Onion Gravy
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, british, comfort food

Golden sausages on creamy mash with a sweet, slow-cooked onion gravy. Pure comfort.

## Ingredients
- 8 pork sausages
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 40g butter
- 100ml milk
- 300g frozen peas
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the onion gravy
- 15g butter
- 2 onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 400ml beef stock
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar

## Method
1. For the gravy, melt the butter in a pan and cook the onions gently for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and golden. Add the sugar for the last 5 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Toss the sausages in the oil and roast for 25–30 minutes, turning halfway, until browned and cooked through.
3. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender. Drain and mash with the butter and milk. Season.
4. Stir the flour into the onions, then gradually add the stock and vinegar. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick.
5. Cook the peas for 3 minutes. Serve the sausages on the mash with the peas and plenty of gravy.

## Notes
- Any sausages work. Try Cumberland or pork and apple.
