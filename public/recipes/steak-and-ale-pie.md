# Steak & Ale Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 3 hr
Tags: dinner, pie, british, comfort food

Slow-cooked beef in a rich ale gravy under a golden puff pastry lid. Proper pub food at home.

## Ingredients
- 800g braising steak, cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, chopped
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, halved
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 500ml brown ale
- 300ml beef stock
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 320g puff pastry, ready rolled
- 1 egg, beaten
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Toss the beef in the flour with some seasoning. Heat the oil in a large casserole and brown the beef in batches. Lift out.
2. Add the onions and mushrooms and cook for 8 minutes. Add the garlic and tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Return the beef, pour in the ale and stock, and add the thyme and bay leaf. Cover and simmer gently for 2 hours until the beef is tender. Cool a little.
4. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Spoon the filling into a pie dish, cover with the pastry, crimp the edges and brush with egg.
5. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the pastry is puffed and golden.

## Notes
- Make the filling a day ahead: it tastes even better.
- Serve with mash and peas.
