# Tuna Niçoise Salad
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: lunch, quick

A hearty French salad with tuna, new potatoes, green beans, eggs and olives in a mustardy dressing.

## Ingredients
- 500g new potatoes, halved
- 200g green beans, trimmed
- 4 eggs
- 2 tins tuna (145g each), drained
- 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 little gem lettuces, leaves separated
- 75g black olives

### For the dressing
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 clove garlic, crushed

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes until tender, adding the beans for the last 4 minutes. Drain and cool.
2. Boil the eggs for 7 minutes, then cool in cold water, peel and halve.
3. Whisk the dressing ingredients with a pinch of salt.
4. Arrange the lettuce, potatoes, beans, tomatoes, olives and tuna on a platter. Top with the eggs and drizzle with the dressing.

## Notes
- Use a seared fresh tuna steak for a special version.
