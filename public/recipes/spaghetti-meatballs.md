# Spaghetti & Meatballs
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, family

Tender beef meatballs simmered in a simple tomato sauce and piled on spaghetti. A guaranteed family favourite.

## Ingredients
- 500g beef mince
- 50g breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 700g passata
- 15g fresh basil
- 350g spaghetti
- 30g parmesan, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mix the mince, breadcrumbs, egg, half the garlic, the oregano and some seasoning. Roll into about 20 meatballs.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and brown the meatballs all over for 6–8 minutes. Lift out.
3. Add the onion to the pan and cook for 6 minutes, then the rest of the garlic for 1 minute. Pour in the passata and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Return the meatballs and simmer gently for 15 minutes until cooked through. Tear in the basil and season.
5. Cook the spaghetti following the pack instructions, then drain and serve topped with the meatballs, sauce and parmesan.

## Notes
- Make a double batch of meatballs and freeze half, uncooked.
