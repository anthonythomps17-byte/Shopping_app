# Lamb Keema Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, curry, quick

Spiced lamb mince with peas in a rich tomato sauce. A quick, warming curry that's great with naan.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 500g lamb mince
- 2 tbsp medium curry powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 200g frozen peas
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 4 naan breads

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
2. Add the mince and cook, breaking it up, until browned. Spoon off any excess fat.
3. Stir in the curry powder and cumin for 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and 100ml water. Simmer for 15 minutes until thick.
4. Add the peas and garam masala and cook for 3 minutes more. Season.
5. Warm the naan. Serve the keema scattered with coriander, with yoghurt and naan.

## Notes
- Beef or turkey mince works too.
