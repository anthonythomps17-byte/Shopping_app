# Crispy Chilli Beef
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, chinese, fakeaway

Crunchy strips of beef tossed in a sticky, sweet and spicy sauce with peppers and spring onions. A takeaway favourite.

## Ingredients
- 500g rump steak, cut into thin strips
- 4 tbsp cornflour
- 4 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 300g basmati rice

### For the sauce
- 4 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp tomato ketchup

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions. Mix the sauce ingredients.
2. Toss the beef in the cornflour with a pinch of salt until well coated.
3. Heat 3 tbsp of the oil in a wok over a high heat and fry the beef in batches for 2–3 minutes until crisp. Drain on kitchen paper.
4. Wipe out the wok, add the rest of the oil and stir fry the pepper and onion for 3 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and chilli for 1 minute.
5. Pour in the sauce and bubble for 1 minute, then toss in the beef and most of the spring onions. Serve straight away on the rice, topped with the rest of the spring onions.

## Notes
- Serve as soon as it's ready, while the beef is still crisp.
