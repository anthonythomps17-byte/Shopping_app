# Sticky Hoisin Pork Noodles
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, quick, chinese

Crispy pork mince in a sweet and sticky hoisin sauce with noodles and greens. Ready in 20 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 500g pork mince
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 250g egg noodles
- 2 pak choi, quartered
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Method
1. Cook the noodles following the pack instructions. Add the pak choi for the last minute, then drain.
2. Heat the oil in a wok over a high heat. Add the mince and fry, breaking it up, for 8 minutes until browned and crispy.
3. Add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then stir in the hoisin, soy sauce and vinegar and bubble for 1 minute until sticky.
4. Toss in the noodles and pak choi. Serve topped with the spring onions, chilli and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Turkey or chicken mince works too.
