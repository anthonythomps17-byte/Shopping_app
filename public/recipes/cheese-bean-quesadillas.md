# Cheese & Bean Quesadillas
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, mexican, quick, vegetarian

Crispy toasted tortillas filled with spiced beans, sweetcorn and melted cheese, with salsa and soured cream for dipping.

## Ingredients
- 1 tin black beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 8 flour tortillas
- 150g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 150ml soured cream
- 1 jar salsa (about 300g)

## Method
1. Roughly mash the beans with the spices, then stir in the sweetcorn and spring onions.
2. Spread the beans over 4 tortillas, scatter with the cheese and top with the remaining tortillas.
3. Heat a little oil in a large frying pan and cook each quesadilla for 2–3 minutes per side until crisp and the cheese has melted.
4. Cut into wedges and serve with soured cream and salsa.

## Notes
- Add sliced peppers or leftover roasted veg to the filling.
