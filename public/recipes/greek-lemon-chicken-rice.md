# Greek Lemon Chicken & Rice
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, greek, one pan, family

Golden chicken thighs baked on lemony, garlicky rice with oregano. One pan, very little effort.

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken thighs, skin on and bone in
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 300g long grain rice
- 700ml chicken stock
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 150g frozen peas
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Season the chicken.
2. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof pan and brown the chicken skin-side down for 8 minutes. Lift out.
3. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, then the garlic and oregano for 1 minute. Stir in the rice to coat.
4. Pour in the stock with the lemon zest and juice. Bring to the boil, then sit the chicken on top, skin-side up.
5. Bake for 30 minutes until the rice is tender and the chicken is cooked through. Stir the peas into the rice for the last 5 minutes.
6. Scatter with parsley and serve from the pan.

## Notes
- No ovenproof pan? Transfer everything to a roasting tin before it goes in the oven.
