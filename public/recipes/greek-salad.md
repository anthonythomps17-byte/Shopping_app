# Greek Salad
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, greek, vegetarian

Juicy tomatoes, crunchy cucumber, peppers, olives and a slab of feta with oregano and olive oil. Serve with warm pittas.

## Ingredients
- 4 tomatoes, cut into chunks
- 1 cucumber, cut into chunks
- 1 green pepper, sliced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 75g black olives
- 200g feta
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 4 pittas
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Put the tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, onion and olives in a large bowl.
2. Whisk the oil and vinegar with a pinch of salt and pepper and toss through the salad.
3. Top with the feta, broken into large chunks, and sprinkle with oregano.
4. Serve with warm pittas.

## Notes
- Add a tin of drained chickpeas to make it more filling.
