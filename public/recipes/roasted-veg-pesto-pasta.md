# Roasted Veg Pesto Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, easy, vegetarian

Sweet roasted peppers, courgette and tomatoes tossed with pasta, pesto and fresh rocket.

## Ingredients
- 2 red peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 courgette, cut into chunks
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 350g fusilli
- 5 tbsp green pesto, vegetarian
- 60g rocket
- 30g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7. Toss the peppers, courgette and onion with the oil and seasoning and roast for 15 minutes. Add the tomatoes and roast for 10 minutes more.
2. Meanwhile, cook the fusilli following the pack instructions. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
3. Toss the pasta with the pesto, roasted vegetables and a splash of the cooking water.
4. Serve topped with rocket and the cheese.

## Notes
- Most shop-bought pesto contains parmesan, so check the label for a vegetarian one.
