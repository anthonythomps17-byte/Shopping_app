# Currywurst & Chips
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, german, street food, quick, family, fakeaway

Berlin's favourite street snack: sliced bratwurst smothered in a sweet, spicy curry ketchup, served with a pile of chips and a dusting of curry powder.

## Ingredients
- 8 bratwurst sausages
- 750g frozen oven chips
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp curry powder, plus extra to serve
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 250g ketchup
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cider vinegar
- 100ml water
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a small pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion for 6–8 minutes until soft.
3. Stir in the curry powder and paprika for 1 minute, then add the tomato purée, ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, sugar, vinegar and water. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth if you like.
4. Fry or grill the bratwurst for 10–12 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
5. Slice the sausages into thick rounds, pile onto plates with the chips, spoon over the warm sauce and dust with a little more curry powder.

## Notes
- Any good pork sausages work if you can't find bratwurst.
- The sauce keeps in the fridge for a week; it's great with burgers and nuggets too.
