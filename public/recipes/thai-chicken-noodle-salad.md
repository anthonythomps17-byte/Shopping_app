# Thai Chicken Noodle Salad
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, quick, thai

Rice noodles, shredded chicken and crunchy vegetables tossed in a zingy lime, chilli and fish sauce dressing.

## Ingredients
- 200g flat rice noodles
- 2 cooked chicken breasts (about 300g), shredded
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- ½ cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- 30g roasted peanuts, chopped

### For the dressing
- 2 limes, juiced
- 2 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Method
1. Cook the noodles following the pack instructions, then rinse in cold water and drain well.
2. Whisk the dressing ingredients together.
3. Toss the noodles with the chicken, carrot, cucumber, pepper, spring onions, herbs and dressing.
4. Serve scattered with peanuts.

## Notes
- Keeps well in a lunchbox: add the peanuts just before eating.
