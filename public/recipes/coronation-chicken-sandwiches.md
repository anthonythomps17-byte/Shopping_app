# Coronation Chicken Sandwiches
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, british

Creamy, gently spiced chicken with mango chutney and sultanas, piled into soft rolls. Perfect for picnics.

## Ingredients
- 2 cooked chicken breasts (about 300g), shredded
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 3 tbsp natural yoghurt
- 2 tsp medium curry powder
- 2 tbsp mango chutney
- 30g sultanas
- 20g flaked almonds, toasted
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 4 soft rolls
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mix the mayonnaise, yoghurt, curry powder, mango chutney and lemon juice.
2. Stir in the chicken, sultanas and most of the almonds, then season.
3. Split the rolls and fill with lettuce and the chicken. Scatter with the rest of the almonds.

## Notes
- A great way to use up leftover roast chicken.
- Also good on a jacket potato or with salad.
