# Chicken Gyros
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, greek, street food, quick, fakeaway

Garlicky, lemony chicken thighs charred in a hot pan and wrapped up with tzatziki and salad in soft flatbreads. A quick shop-bought tzatziki keeps it a weeknight job.

## Ingredients
- 700g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 4 flatbreads
- 200g tzatziki
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 3 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 50g feta, crumbled
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mix the oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, paprika and coriander with plenty of salt and pepper. Coat the chicken in it and leave for at least 15 minutes, or up to a day in the fridge.
2. Heat a griddle or large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 5–6 minutes per side until charred and cooked through. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice thinly.
3. Warm the flatbreads in a dry pan.
4. Spread each flatbread with tzatziki, then top with lettuce, tomatoes, onion, chicken and feta. Fold up and wrap the bottom in foil or baking paper to eat.

## Notes
- For the full Greek takeaway experience, tuck a few oven chips inside too.
- The marinated chicken also cooks well on the barbecue.
