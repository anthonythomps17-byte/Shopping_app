# Tomato & Mozzarella Gnocchi Bake
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, italian, quick, one pan, vegetarian

Pillowy gnocchi baked in a rich tomato and spinach sauce with bubbling mozzarella. Ready in half an hour.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 800g gnocchi
- 100g baby spinach
- 125g mozzarella, torn
- 30g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- 15g fresh basil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the grill to high. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof frying pan and cook the onion for 6 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Add the tomatoes, purée, oregano and 100ml water and simmer for 8 minutes. Season.
3. Stir in the gnocchi and spinach and cook for 3–4 minutes until the gnocchi are tender and the spinach has wilted.
4. Scatter over the mozzarella and hard cheese and grill for 5 minutes until golden and bubbling. Top with basil.

## Notes
- No ovenproof pan? Tip everything into a baking dish before grilling.
