# Smashed Avocado & Poached Eggs on Toast
Serves: 2
Time: 15 min
Tags: breakfast, brunch, quick, vegetarian

Crushed avocado with lemon and chilli on toasted sourdough, topped with soft poached eggs.

## Ingredients
- 2 avocados
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 1 pinch chilli flakes
- 4 eggs
- 1 tsp white wine vinegar
- 4 slices sourdough
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mash the avocados with the lemon juice, chilli flakes and some seasoning.
2. Bring a pan of water to a gentle simmer and add the vinegar. Crack in the eggs one at a time and poach for 3 minutes until the whites are set.
3. Meanwhile, toast the sourdough.
4. Spread the toast with the avocado, top with the eggs and finish with more chilli flakes and black pepper.

## Notes
- Crack each egg into a cup first, then slide it into the water: they hold their shape better.
