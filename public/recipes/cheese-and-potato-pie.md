# Cheese & Potato Pie
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, british, family, comfort food, vegetarian

A Lancashire favourite: creamy mashed potato, onion and cheese baked under a crisp pastry lid. Very cheap and very filling.

## Ingredients
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 25g butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 100ml milk
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 150g cheddar cheese, grated
- 320g shortcrust pastry, ready rolled
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tin baked beans (415g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender. Meanwhile, fry the onion in the butter for 8 minutes until soft.
2. Drain and mash the potatoes with the onion, milk, mustard and cheese. Season well.
3. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Spoon the filling into a pie dish, cover with the pastry, crimp the edges and brush with egg.
4. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden. Serve with the beans.

## Notes
- Most supermarket cheddar is vegetarian, but check the label.
