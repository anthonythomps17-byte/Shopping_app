# Black Bean Chilli
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, mexican, batch cook, vegetarian

A smoky, rich chilli made with black beans, peppers and sweetcorn. Great for batch cooking.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 1 yellow pepper, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tins black beans (400g each), drained and rinsed
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 250ml vegetable stock
- 10g dark chocolate, optional
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 300g long grain rice
- 150ml soured cream
- 100g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion and peppers for 8–10 minutes until soft, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Stir in the paprika, cumin, chilli powder, oregano and tomato purée and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the black beans, chopped tomatoes, sweetcorn and stock. Bring to a simmer and cook, uncovered, for 20–25 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.
4. Meanwhile, cook the rice following the pack instructions.
5. Stir in the chocolate, if using, until melted. Season to taste.
6. Serve over the rice with soured cream, cheddar, coriander and lime wedges.

## Notes
- Freezes well for up to 3 months.
- Also good in jacket potatoes or wrapped in tortillas.
- Use kidney beans or a mix of beans if that's what you have.
