# Swedish Meatballs
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, comfort food, family

Juicy beef meatballs in a creamy gravy with buttery mash and a spoonful of cranberry sauce.

## Ingredients
- 500g beef mince
- 50g breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 onion, very finely chopped
- ½ tsp ground allspice
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 50g butter
- 75ml milk
- 4 tbsp cranberry sauce
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the gravy
- 25g butter
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 400ml beef stock
- 100ml double cream
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

## Method
1. Mix the mince, breadcrumbs, egg, onion, allspice and plenty of seasoning. Roll into about 24 small meatballs.
2. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender, then mash with the butter and milk.
3. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large frying pan and brown the meatballs all over for 8–10 minutes. Lift out.
4. For the gravy, melt the butter in the pan, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the stock. Add the cream, soy sauce and mustard and simmer for 3 minutes.
5. Return the meatballs and simmer for 5 minutes until cooked through. Serve with the mash and cranberry sauce.

## Notes
- Lingonberry jam is traditional if you can find it.
