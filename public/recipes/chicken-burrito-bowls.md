# Chicken Burrito Bowls
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, mexican, quick

Spiced chicken, lime and coriander rice, black beans and all the toppings, in a bowl. Easy to make your own.

## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g), sliced
- 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 300g long grain rice
- 2 limes, 1 juiced, 1 cut into wedges
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 tin black beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 250g cherry tomatoes, quartered
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 150ml soured cream
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions, then stir in the lime juice and half the coriander.
2. Toss the chicken with the fajita seasoning and oil. Cook in a hot frying pan for 6–8 minutes until charred and cooked through.
3. Warm the beans and sweetcorn together in a small pan.
4. Divide the rice between bowls and top with the chicken, beans and sweetcorn, avocado, tomatoes, onion and lettuce.
5. Finish with soured cream, cheese, the rest of the coriander and lime wedges.

## Notes
- Add salsa or hot sauce if you like it spicy.
