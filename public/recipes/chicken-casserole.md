# Chicken Casserole
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, british, family, comfort food

A hearty, warming one-pot of chicken thighs, potatoes and vegetables in a savoury gravy. Cheap and very forgiving.

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 carrots, cut into chunks
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, halved
- 500g potatoes, cut into chunks
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 500ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 150g frozen peas
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Toss the chicken in the flour with some seasoning. Heat the oil in a large casserole and brown the chicken all over. Lift out.
2. Add the onion, carrots and mushrooms and cook for 8 minutes. Add the garlic and tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Return the chicken with the potatoes, stock, mustard and thyme. Cover and simmer gently for 40 minutes until the chicken and potatoes are tender.
4. Stir in the peas for the last 5 minutes. Season and serve.

## Notes
- Freezes well. Serve with crusty bread to mop up the gravy.
