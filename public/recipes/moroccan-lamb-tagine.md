# Moroccan Lamb Tagine
Serves: 4
Time: 2 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, slow cook, middle eastern

Slow-cooked lamb with warm spices, chickpeas and sweet apricots, served with herby couscous.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 700g diced lamb shoulder
- 2 onions, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp ras el hanout
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin chickpeas (400g), drained
- 100g dried apricots, halved
- 400ml lamb stock
- 1 tbsp honey
- 250g couscous
- 30g flaked almonds, toasted
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large casserole and brown the lamb in batches. Lift out.
2. Add the onions and cook for 10 minutes until soft. Add the garlic, ginger and spices for 1 minute.
3. Return the lamb with the tomatoes, chickpeas, apricots, stock and honey. Cover and simmer gently for 1 hr 30 min, until the lamb is tender. Season.
4. Put the couscous in a bowl, pour over 300ml boiling water, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff up with a fork.
5. Serve the tagine on the couscous, scattered with almonds and coriander.

## Notes
- Ras el hanout is a Moroccan spice blend, found with the other spices.
