# Thai Red Prawn Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, curry, thai, quick

Juicy prawns and crunchy vegetables in a fragrant red coconut curry. Faster than a takeaway.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp soft brown sugar
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 150g mangetout
- 300g king prawns, raw and peeled
- 1 lime, juiced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 300g jasmine rice

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Heat the oil in a wok and fry the curry paste for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the coconut milk and 100ml water, add the fish sauce and sugar, and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Add the pepper and mangetout and cook for 3 minutes, then the prawns for 2–3 minutes until pink.
5. Stir in the lime juice and most of the coriander. Serve on the rice with the rest of the coriander.

## Notes
- Use chicken thighs instead of prawns: simmer them for 10 minutes before adding the vegetables.
