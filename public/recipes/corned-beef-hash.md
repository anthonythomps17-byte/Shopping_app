# Corned Beef Hash
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, british, easy, one pan

A thrifty classic: crispy potatoes, onion and corned beef fried together and topped with fried eggs.

## Ingredients
- 800g potatoes, cut into 2cm cubes
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 tin corned beef (340g), cut into cubes
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 4 eggs
- 1 tin baked beans (415g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 6 minutes until just tender, then drain well.
2. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large frying pan and fry the potatoes for 10 minutes, turning, until golden. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes more.
3. Add the corned beef and Worcestershire sauce and stir gently, pressing down, for 5 minutes until crispy in places. Season.
4. Meanwhile, warm the beans and fry the eggs in the rest of the oil.
5. Serve the hash topped with the eggs, with the beans on the side.

## Notes
- Chill the tin of corned beef first so it's easier to cut.
