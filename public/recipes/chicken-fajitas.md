# Chicken Fajitas
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, mexican, quick, family

Sizzling spiced chicken with peppers and onions, wrapped in warm tortillas with all the toppings. Everyone builds their own.

## Ingredients
- 3 chicken breasts (about 450g), sliced
- 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 1 green pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 8 flour tortillas
- 150ml soured cream
- 150g guacamole
- 1 jar salsa (about 300g)
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Toss the chicken with the fajita seasoning and 1 tbsp of the oil.
2. Heat the rest of the oil in a large frying pan over a high heat and cook the peppers and onion for 5 minutes until charred at the edges. Lift out.
3. Add the chicken and cook for 6–7 minutes until golden and cooked through. Return the vegetables and toss together.
4. Warm the tortillas and serve with the chicken, soured cream, guacamole, salsa, cheese and lime wedges.

## Notes
- Use the same method with prawns, steak or halloumi.
