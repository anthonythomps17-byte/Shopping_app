# Turkish Lahmacun
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, turkish, street food, quick

Thin, crisp flatbreads spread with spiced lamb, tomato and pepper, then topped with parsley, red onion and lemon and rolled up to eat. Tortilla wraps make a brilliant shortcut base.

## Ingredients
- 400g lamb mince
- 1 onion, very finely chopped
- 1 red pepper, very finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, very finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 30g fresh parsley, chopped
- 8 tortilla wraps
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ground sumac
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7.
2. Mix the lamb with the onion, pepper, tomatoes, garlic, tomato purée, spices, half the parsley and plenty of salt and pepper, squishing it together until it's a soft paste.
3. Lay the wraps on baking trays and spread the lamb thinly right to the edges.
4. Bake for 8–10 minutes, in batches if you need to, until the lamb is cooked and the edges are crisp.
5. Toss the red onion with the sumac and the rest of the parsley. Scatter over the lahmacun with the lettuce, squeeze over some lemon and roll up to eat.

## Notes
- Beef mince works just as well.
- They reheat well in a hot oven for lunch the next day.
