# Salmon Teriyaki
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, quick, japanese

Sticky glazed salmon fillets with rice and greens. Quick, healthy and full of flavour.

## Ingredients
- 4 salmon fillets
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 tbsp teriyaki sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 300g basmati rice
- 2 pak choi, halved
- 150g tenderstem broccoli
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Mix the teriyaki sauce and honey.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Cook the salmon skin-side down for 4 minutes until crisp, then turn and cook for 2 minutes more.
4. Pour in the sauce and bubble for 1 minute, spooning it over the salmon until glossy.
5. Meanwhile, steam the pak choi and broccoli for 3–4 minutes.
6. Serve the salmon on the rice with the greens, topped with spring onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Also works with chicken thighs: cook them through before adding the sauce.
