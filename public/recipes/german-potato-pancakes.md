# German Potato Pancakes
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, lunch, german, street food, vegetarian, family

Kartoffelpuffer, the crispy potato pancakes from German Christmas markets, served with a quick homemade apple sauce and soured cream.

## Ingredients
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled
- 1 onion
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 4 tbsp plain flour
- ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
- 6 tbsp vegetable oil
- 150ml soured cream
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the apple sauce
- 4 eating apples, peeled, cored and chopped
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon

### To serve
- 120g mixed salad leaves

## Method
1. Put the apples, sugar, lemon juice, cinnamon and 3 tbsp water in a small pan. Cover and cook gently for 10 minutes until soft, then mash. Leave to cool.
2. Coarsely grate the potatoes and onion. Tip into a clean tea towel and squeeze out as much liquid as you can.
3. Mix with the eggs, flour, nutmeg, 1 tsp salt and some pepper.
4. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Drop in heaped spoonfuls of the mixture, flatten to about 1cm thick and fry for 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp. Drain on kitchen paper and keep warm while you cook the rest, adding more oil as needed.
5. Serve hot with the apple sauce, soured cream sprinkled with chives, and the salad.

## Notes
- Keep the cooked pancakes crisp on a rack in a low oven.
- For a meat eater, they're great with bacon or smoked salmon on top.
