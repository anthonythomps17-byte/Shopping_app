# Prawn Laksa
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, soup, quick

A fragrant, spicy coconut noodle soup with prawns, beansprouts and lime.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 tbsp laksa paste
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 750ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp soft brown sugar
- 200g flat rice noodles
- 300g king prawns, raw and peeled
- 2 pak choi, quartered
- 150g beansprouts
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the laksa paste for 1 minute.
2. Add the coconut milk, stock, fish sauce and sugar and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, cook the noodles following the pack instructions, then drain and divide between bowls.
4. Add the prawns and pak choi to the soup and simmer for 3 minutes until the prawns are pink.
5. Ladle over the noodles and top with beansprouts, spring onions, chilli, coriander and lime wedges.

## Notes
- Use Thai red curry paste if you can't find laksa paste.
