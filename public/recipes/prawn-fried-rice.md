# Prawn Fried Rice
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, quick, chinese, fakeaway

Prawns, egg and veg tossed with soy-coated rice. A cheap and speedy fakeaway using frozen prawns.

## Ingredients
- 300g basmati rice, cooked and cooled (or 750g cooked rice)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 250g frozen cooked prawns, defrosted
- 150g frozen peas
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a wok over a high heat. Scramble the eggs until just set, then lift out.
2. Add the rest of the oil, then the peas, sweetcorn, garlic and most of the spring onions and stir fry for 2 minutes.
3. Add the rice and stir fry for 3 minutes until piping hot, then add the prawns for 1–2 minutes to heat through.
4. Return the eggs, add the soy sauce and sesame oil and toss well. Serve topped with the rest of the spring onions.

## Notes
- Cold, day-old rice fries best.
