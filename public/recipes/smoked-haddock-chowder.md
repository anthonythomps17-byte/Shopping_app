# Smoked Haddock Chowder
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, soup, comfort food

A thick, creamy soup with smoked haddock, potatoes and sweetcorn. Warming and filling.

## Ingredients
- 25g butter
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 leeks, sliced
- 500g potatoes, peeled and cut into small cubes
- 500ml vegetable stock
- 400ml milk
- 1 bay leaf
- 400g smoked haddock fillets, skinned and cut into chunks
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 100ml double cream
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the onion and leeks for 8 minutes until soft.
2. Add the potatoes, stock, milk and bay leaf. Simmer for 12–15 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
3. Add the haddock and sweetcorn and simmer gently for 5 minutes until the fish flakes.
4. Stir in the cream and parsley and season. Serve with crusty bread.

## Notes
- Don't let it boil hard once the milk is in.
