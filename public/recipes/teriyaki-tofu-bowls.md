# Teriyaki Tofu Bowls
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, quick, japanese, vegetarian, vegan

Crispy tofu in a sticky teriyaki glaze with rice, edamame, crunchy veg and avocado.

## Ingredients
- 400g firm tofu, patted dry and cubed
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 5 tbsp teriyaki sauce
- 300g basmati rice
- 150g frozen edamame beans
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions. Cook the edamame in boiling water for 3 minutes, then drain.
2. Toss the tofu in the cornflour. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and fry the tofu for 8–10 minutes, turning, until crisp all over.
3. Pour in the teriyaki sauce and toss for 1 minute until sticky.
4. Divide the rice between bowls and top with the tofu, edamame, carrot, cucumber and avocado. Scatter with spring onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Check your teriyaki sauce: most are vegan, but some contain honey.
