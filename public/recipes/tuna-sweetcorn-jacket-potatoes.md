# Tuna & Sweetcorn Jacket Potatoes
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, family, easy

Fluffy jacket potatoes loaded with creamy tuna and sweetcorn and melted cheese. Cheap, filling and almost no effort.

## Ingredients
- 4 potatoes (for baking)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tins tuna (145g each), drained
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 bag mixed salad leaves (about 120g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Prick the potatoes, rub with the oil and some salt, and bake for 1 hour until crisp outside and soft inside.
2. Mix the tuna, sweetcorn, mayonnaise and spring onions, and season.
3. Split the potatoes, fill with the tuna, top with the cheese and return to the oven for 5 minutes to melt.
4. Serve with the salad.

## Notes
- Short on time? Microwave the potatoes for 8–10 minutes, then crisp them in the oven for 15.
