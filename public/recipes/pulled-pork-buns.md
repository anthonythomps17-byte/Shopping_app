# Pulled Pork Buns
Serves: 6
Time: 4 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, bbq, slow cook, batch cook

Pork shoulder slow-roasted with a smoky spice rub until it falls apart, then tossed in barbecue sauce and piled into buns with slaw.

## Ingredients
- 1.5kg pork shoulder, boneless, rind removed
- 1 onion, sliced
- 100ml cider vinegar
- 150ml barbecue sauce
- 6 brioche buns
- 300g coleslaw

### For the rub
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 150°C (130°C fan), gas 2. Mix the rub ingredients and massage all over the pork.
2. Put the onion in a roasting tin, sit the pork on top, and pour 200ml water and the vinegar around it. Cover tightly with foil.
3. Roast for 4 hours, until the pork pulls apart easily with a fork.
4. Shred the pork with two forks, discarding any fat, and mix with the barbecue sauce and some of the juices from the tin.
5. Pile into the toasted buns and top with coleslaw.

## Notes
- In a slow cooker: cook on low for 8 hours.
- Freezes well. Leftovers are great in wraps or on jacket potatoes.
