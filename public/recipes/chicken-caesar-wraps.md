# Chicken Caesar Wraps
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: lunch, quick

Griddled chicken, crunchy lettuce and garlicky croutons in a creamy Caesar dressing, rolled up in tortillas. Good for packed lunches too.

## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 4 flour tortillas (large)
- 2 little gem lettuces, shredded
- 30g parmesan, shaved
- 2 slices bread, cut into small cubes
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the dressing
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 15g parmesan, finely grated
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- ½ lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Toss the bread cubes with half the oil and a pinch of salt and bake for 8–10 minutes until golden.
2. Bash the chicken breasts between two sheets of cling film until about 1.5cm thick. Rub with the rest of the oil and season.
3. Cook the chicken in a hot griddle or frying pan for 4–5 minutes per side until cooked through. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
4. Whisk all the dressing ingredients together with 1 tbsp water.
5. Toss the lettuce with most of the dressing. Warm the tortillas, then fill with the lettuce, chicken, croutons and shaved parmesan. Drizzle over the rest of the dressing, roll up and serve.

## Notes
- Leftover roast chicken works well here.
- For lunchboxes, keep the dressing separate until you're ready to eat.
