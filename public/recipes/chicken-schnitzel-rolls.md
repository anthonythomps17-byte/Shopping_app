# Chicken Schnitzel Rolls
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, lunch, german, street food, quick, family

Crisp, golden breaded chicken in soft rolls with mustard mayo, lettuce, tomato and gherkins, the way they're sold at Austrian and German market stalls.

## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breasts
- 50g plain flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 100g panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 4 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 crusty white rolls
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 4 gherkins, sliced
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cut each chicken breast in half horizontally to make 4 thin fillets. Put them between sheets of baking paper and bash with a rolling pin until about 5mm thick.
2. Put the flour, seasoned with salt, pepper and the paprika, in one shallow bowl, the eggs in another and the breadcrumbs in a third. Coat each fillet in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Fry the schnitzels for 3 minutes per side, in batches if you need to, until golden and cooked through. Drain on kitchen paper.
4. Mix the mayonnaise and mustard. Split the rolls and spread with the mustard mayo.
5. Fill with lettuce, tomato, a schnitzel and some gherkins. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Pork loin steaks, bashed thin, make a classic pork schnitzel.
- Serve on a plate with potato salad and a lemon wedge instead of in a roll.
