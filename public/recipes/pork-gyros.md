# Pork Gyros
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, greek, street food, fakeaway

The Greek takeaway classic: lemony, oregano-spiced pork strips crisped in a hot pan, wrapped in warm flatbreads with tzatziki, tomato, red onion and a few chips tucked inside.

## Ingredients
- 800g pork shoulder steaks, cut into thin strips
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 600g potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), cut into thin chips
- 4 flatbreads (Greek-style pitta breads if you can find them)
- 3 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the tzatziki
- 250g Greek yoghurt
- ½ cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 15g fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar

## Method
1. Mix 2 tbsp of the oil with the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, paprika, cumin and plenty of salt and pepper. Toss the pork in it and leave for at least 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.
2. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7. Toss the chips with the rest of the oil and some salt, spread out on a large tray and roast for 30–35 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.
3. Stir together everything for the tzatziki with a pinch of salt.
4. Heat a large frying pan over a high heat. Fry the pork in two batches for 6–8 minutes each, without moving it too much, until browned and crisp at the edges.
5. Warm the flatbreads in a dry pan or the oven. Spread each with tzatziki, then pile on the pork, tomatoes, onion, a handful of chips and the parsley. Wrap tightly in foil or baking paper to eat.

## Notes
- Boneless pork shoulder or neck stays juicy; leaner pork loin dries out quicker.
- Or serve it as a plate: pork and chips with the flatbreads, tzatziki and a Greek salad on the side.
