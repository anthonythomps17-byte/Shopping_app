# Homemade Margherita Pizza
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, italian, family, vegetarian

Easy homemade pizza dough topped with a simple tomato sauce, mozzarella and fresh basil. Fun to make with the kids.

## Ingredients
- 500g strong white bread flour
- 7g fast-action dried yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 300ml water, warm
- 250g passata
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 250g mozzarella, torn
- 15g fresh basil

## Method
1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt in a large bowl. Add 1 tbsp of the oil and the warm water and mix to a soft dough.
2. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth, then cover and leave somewhere warm for 1 hour until doubled in size.
3. Heat the oven as high as it will go (at least 240°C, 220°C fan, gas 9) with two baking trays inside.
4. Mix the passata, garlic, oregano and the rest of the oil with a pinch of salt.
5. Divide the dough into 4 and roll each out thinly. Spread with the sauce and top with the mozzarella.
6. Slide onto the hot trays and bake for 8–10 minutes until crisp and bubbling. Scatter with basil to serve.

## Notes
- Add your favourite toppings: mushrooms, peppers, olives or sweetcorn.
- The dough freezes well after the first rise.
