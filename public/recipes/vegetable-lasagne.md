# Roasted Vegetable Lasagne
Serves: 6
Time: 1 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, family, vegetarian

Layers of roasted Mediterranean vegetables, rich tomato sauce, creamy white sauce and pasta.

## Ingredients
- 1 aubergine, cut into chunks
- 2 courgettes, cut into chunks
- 2 red peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 red onion, cut into chunks
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 15g fresh basil
- 250g lasagne sheets
- 125g mozzarella, torn
- 40g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the white sauce
- 50g butter
- 50g plain flour
- 600ml milk
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Toss the vegetables with the oil and seasoning and roast for 30 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, simmer the garlic, tomatoes, purée and oregano for 15 minutes. Stir in the roasted vegetables and basil.
3. For the white sauce, melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick. Season with salt and nutmeg.
4. Layer a third of the vegetable sauce, a layer of lasagne sheets and a quarter of the white sauce in a baking dish. Repeat twice, then finish with the rest of the white sauce.
5. Top with the mozzarella and hard cheese and bake for 40 minutes until golden. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

## Notes
- Freezes well, before or after baking.
