# Pork & Apple Burgers
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, bbq, family, quick

Juicy pork burgers with grated apple and sage, in toasted buns with coleslaw.

## Ingredients
- 500g pork mince
- 1 apple, grated
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp dried sage
- 50g breadcrumbs
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 brioche buns
- 1 little gem lettuce, leaves separated
- 300g coleslaw
- 750g frozen oven chips
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
2. Mix the pork, apple, onion, sage, breadcrumbs and some seasoning. Shape into 4 burgers.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the burgers for 6–7 minutes per side until cooked through (or use the barbecue).
4. Toast the buns and fill with lettuce, the burgers and coleslaw.

## Notes
- Pork mince must be cooked all the way through, with no pink.
