# Beef Stew & Dumplings
Serves: 4
Time: 3 hr
Tags: dinner, british, comfort food, slow cook

Slow-cooked beef in a rich gravy with root vegetables, topped with fluffy herb dumplings.

## Ingredients
- 800g braising steak, cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, chopped
- 3 carrots, cut into chunks
- 2 sticks celery, sliced
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 600ml beef stock
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the dumplings
- 150g self-raising flour
- 75g suet
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 160°C (140°C fan), gas 3. Toss the beef in the flour with some salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof casserole and brown the beef in batches. Lift out.
3. Add the onions, carrots and celery and cook for 8 minutes. Stir in the tomato purée, then the stock, Worcestershire sauce, thyme and bay leaves. Return the beef.
4. Cover and cook in the oven for 2 hours, until the beef is very tender.
5. For the dumplings, mix the flour, suet, parsley and a pinch of salt, then add about 100ml cold water to make a soft dough. Shape into 8 balls.
6. Turn the oven up to 190°C (170°C fan), gas 5. Sit the dumplings on top of the stew and cook, uncovered, for 25–30 minutes until risen and golden.

## Notes
- Works in a slow cooker: cook on low for 8 hours, then add the dumplings for the last 45 minutes on high.
