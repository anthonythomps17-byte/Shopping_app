# Sausage & Bean Casserole
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, one pan, comfort food, batch cook

Sausages simmered with butter beans, peppers and smoked paprika in a rich tomato sauce. A cheap and cheerful one-pot.

## Ingredients
- 8 pork sausages
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 2 tins butter beans (400g each), drained
- 300ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large casserole and brown the sausages all over for 8 minutes. Lift out and cut each in half.
2. Add the onion and pepper and cook for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and paprika for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, beans, stock and tomato purée. Return the sausages and simmer for 25 minutes until thick.
4. Season, scatter with parsley and serve with crusty bread.

## Notes
- Use cannellini or haricot beans if that's what you have.
