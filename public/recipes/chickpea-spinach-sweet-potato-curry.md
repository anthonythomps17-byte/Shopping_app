# Chickpea, Spinach & Sweet Potato Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, curry, vegetarian, vegan

A creamy coconut curry packed with sweet potato, chickpeas and spinach. Cheap, filling and even better the next day.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp medium curry powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 sweet potatoes (about 600g), peeled and cut into 2cm chunks
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 2 tins chickpeas (400g each), drained and rinsed
- 160g baby spinach
- 1 lime, juiced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- Salt, to taste

### To serve
- 300g basmati rice

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Fry the onion for 8 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
2. Stir in the curry powder, cumin and turmeric and cook for 1 minute more.
3. Add the sweet potato, chopped tomatoes, coconut milk and chickpeas, plus 150ml water. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook for 15–20 minutes until the sweet potato is tender.
4. Meanwhile, cook the rice following the pack instructions.
5. Stir the spinach into the curry until wilted, then add the lime juice and season with salt.
6. Serve with the rice, scattered with coriander.

## Notes
- Vegan as long as you serve it with rice. Most naan contain milk or yoghurt.
- Keeps in the fridge for 3 days and freezes well.
- Swap the sweet potato for butternut squash.
