# Beef Stroganoff
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, quick, comfort food

Strips of tender steak and mushrooms in a creamy paprika and mustard sauce, served with rice.

## Ingredients
- 500g rump steak, cut into thin strips
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 15g butter
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 200ml beef stock
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 150ml soured cream
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 300g basmati rice
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Toss the steak with the flour, paprika and some seasoning. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large frying pan over a high heat and fry the beef in two batches for 1–2 minutes until browned. Lift out.
3. Add the rest of the oil and the butter. Cook the onion for 6 minutes, then add the mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes until golden. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
4. Pour in the stock and mustard and bubble for 3 minutes.
5. Turn the heat down, stir in the soured cream and return the beef. Heat through without boiling, then scatter with parsley and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Don't overcook the beef: it only needs warming through at the end.
