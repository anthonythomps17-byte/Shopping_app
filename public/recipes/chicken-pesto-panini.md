# Chicken, Pesto & Mozzarella Panini
Serves: 4
Time: 15 min
Tags: lunch, quick, italian

Toasted ciabatta filled with chicken, basil pesto, melting mozzarella and tomato. A café-style lunch at home.

## Ingredients
- 4 ciabatta rolls
- 4 tbsp green pesto
- 2 cooked chicken breasts (about 300g), sliced
- 125g mozzarella, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 60g rocket
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Method
1. Split the ciabatta and spread the insides with pesto.
2. Fill with the chicken, mozzarella and tomato, and season.
3. Brush the outsides with oil and cook in a griddle or frying pan over a medium heat, pressing down with a heavy pan, for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and the cheese has melted.
4. Open up, tuck in some rocket and serve.

## Notes
- A great way to use up leftover roast chicken.
