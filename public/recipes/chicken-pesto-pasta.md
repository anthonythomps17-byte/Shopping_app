# Chicken Pesto Pasta
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, quick, italian

Golden chicken tossed with pasta, basil pesto, juicy cherry tomatoes and spinach. A 20-minute weeknight win.

## Ingredients
- 350g penne
- 2 chicken breasts (about 300g), sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 250g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 80g baby spinach
- 5 tbsp green pesto
- 30g parmesan, finely grated
- 25g pine nuts, toasted
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the penne following the pack instructions. Save a mugful of the cooking water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large frying pan. Season the chicken and fry for 6–7 minutes until golden and cooked through.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes until softening, then stir in the spinach until wilted.
4. Toss in the pasta and pesto with a splash of the pasta water until glossy.
5. Serve topped with the parmesan and pine nuts.

## Notes
- Red pesto works well too.
