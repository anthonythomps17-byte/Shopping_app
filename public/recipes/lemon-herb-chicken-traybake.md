# Lemon & Herb Chicken Traybake
Serves: 4
Time: 55 min
Tags: dinner, traybake, easy

Crispy-skinned chicken thighs roasted with new potatoes, peppers and lemon, all in one tin. Very little washing up.

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken thighs, skin on and bone in
- 750g new potatoes, halved
- 2 red onions, cut into wedges
- 2 red peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 courgette, cut into chunks
- 1 lemon, zested and cut into wedges
- 4 cloves garlic, bashed but unpeeled
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 100g tenderstem broccoli
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7.
2. Put the potatoes, onions, peppers, courgette, garlic and lemon wedges in a large roasting tin. Toss with 2 tbsp of the oil, the oregano and plenty of salt and pepper.
3. Rub the chicken with the rest of the oil, the lemon zest and some salt. Sit the thighs skin-side up on top of the vegetables and tuck in the thyme.
4. Roast for 35 minutes, then add the broccoli around the chicken and roast for another 10–15 minutes, until the chicken skin is crisp and the juices run clear.
5. Squeeze the roasted lemon wedges over everything and serve straight from the tin.

## Notes
- Boneless thighs cook in about 30 minutes in total.
- Swap the broccoli for green beans or asparagus.
- Leftovers make a good lunch, cold or reheated.
