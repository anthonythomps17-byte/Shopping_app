# Vegetable Egg Fried Rice
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, quick, chinese, fakeaway, vegetarian

Better than the takeaway and ready in 20 minutes. A great way to use up leftover rice and odds and ends of veg.

## Ingredients
- 300g basmati rice, cooked and cooled (or 750g cooked rice)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 1 red pepper, finely diced
- 150g frozen peas
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a wok over a high heat. Pour in the eggs, scramble until just set, then lift out.
2. Add the rest of the oil and stir fry the carrots and pepper for 3 minutes. Add the peas, sweetcorn, garlic and most of the spring onions for 1 minute.
3. Add the rice and stir fry for 3–4 minutes until piping hot.
4. Return the eggs, add the soy sauce and sesame oil, and toss well. Serve topped with the rest of the spring onions.

## Notes
- Cold, day-old rice fries best. If you cook rice specially, spread it out to cool quickly and chill it.
