# Lamb Doner Kebabs
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 15 min
Tags: dinner, turkish, street food, fakeaway

A homemade take on the late-night favourite: spiced lamb baked as a loaf, sliced wafer-thin and crisped in a pan, then stuffed into flatbreads with salad, garlic sauce and hot sauce.

## Ingredients
- 500g lamb mince
- 1 onion, grated and squeezed dry
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 flatbreads
- ¼ white cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp hot sauce (or sriracha)

### For the garlic sauce
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan), gas 4.
2. Put the lamb, onion, garlic, spices, oregano, chilli and salt in a bowl and squish together with your hands for 3–4 minutes, until it turns smooth and sticky. This gives the dense doner texture.
3. Press into a 900g (2lb) loaf tin and bake for 50 minutes. Leave to cool for 10 minutes, then lift out and slice as thinly as you can.
4. Meanwhile, mix together the garlic sauce ingredients with a pinch of salt.
5. Heat a large frying pan over a high heat and fry the slices for 1–2 minutes each side until crisp at the edges.
6. Warm the flatbreads, fill with cabbage, lettuce, tomatoes and onion, then the meat. Finish with garlic sauce and as much hot sauce as you like.

## Notes
- The cooked loaf freezes well: slice, freeze, and fry from frozen for an instant kebab night.
- Serve with chips on the side for the full takeaway.
