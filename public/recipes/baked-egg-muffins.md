# Baked Egg Muffins
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: breakfast, easy, family, vegetarian

Little baked omelettes packed with spinach, peppers and cheese. Make a batch for grab-and-go breakfasts all week.

## Ingredients
- 8 eggs
- 50ml milk
- 1 red pepper, finely chopped
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 50g baby spinach, chopped
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan), gas 5. Brush a 12-hole muffin tin with the oil.
2. Divide the pepper, spring onions, spinach and most of the cheese between the holes.
3. Beat the eggs with the milk and some seasoning, then pour over the vegetables. Top with the rest of the cheese.
4. Bake for 18–20 minutes until puffed and just set. Cool for a few minutes before turning out.

## Notes
- Keep in the fridge for 3 days. Eat cold or warm in the microwave for 30 seconds.
