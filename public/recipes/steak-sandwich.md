# Steak Sandwich with Caramelised Onions
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, lunch, quick

Juicy sliced steak with sweet, sticky onions, peppery rocket and horseradish mayo in toasted ciabatta.

## Ingredients
- 2 sirloin steaks
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 15g butter
- 2 onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 4 ciabatta rolls
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp horseradish sauce
- 60g rocket
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Melt the butter in a frying pan and cook the onions gently for 15 minutes until soft and golden. Add the sugar and vinegar for the last 2 minutes until sticky.
2. Meanwhile, rub the steaks with the oil and season. Cook in a very hot pan for 2–3 minutes per side for medium-rare, then rest for 5 minutes and slice thinly.
3. Mix the mayonnaise and horseradish. Split and toast the ciabatta.
4. Spread the ciabatta with the horseradish mayo, then fill with rocket, steak and onions.

## Notes
- Minute steaks or rump work too, and are cheaper.
