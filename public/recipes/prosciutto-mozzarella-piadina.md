# Prosciutto & Mozzarella Piadina
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, italian, street food

Soft, quick Italian flatbreads from Emilia-Romagna, cooked in a dry pan and folded around prosciutto, creamy mozzarella and peppery rocket.

## Ingredients
- 400g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 200ml milk, warm
- 180g prosciutto
- 250g mozzarella, sliced
- 60g rocket
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 tbsp balsamic glaze

## Method
1. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt, then add the oil and milk and bring together into a soft dough. Knead for 3–4 minutes until smooth, cover and rest for 15 minutes.
2. Split the dough into 4 and roll each piece out to a thin round about 25cm across.
3. Heat a large dry frying pan over a medium-high heat. Cook each piadina for 1–2 minutes per side until puffed and spotted with brown.
4. While each one is still hot, lay mozzarella over one half so it starts to melt, then top with prosciutto, tomato and rocket.
5. Drizzle with a little balsamic glaze, fold in half and serve.

## Notes
- Shop-bought tortilla wraps make a quick swap for the homemade bread.
- Try salami, or roasted veg and pesto to make it veggie.
