# Churros with Chocolate Sauce
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: treat, spanish, street food, vegetarian

Crisp, ridged doughnut sticks rolled in cinnamon sugar and dunked in a rich chocolate sauce. Made with a simple choux-style dough and no special kit beyond a piping bag.

## Ingredients
- 250ml water
- 30g butter
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 150g plain flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- 1 litre vegetable oil, for frying
- Salt, to taste

### For the cinnamon sugar
- 75g caster sugar
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon

### For the chocolate sauce
- 100g dark chocolate, chopped
- 150ml double cream

## Method
1. Put the water, butter, sugar and a pinch of salt in a pan and bring to the boil. Take off the heat, tip in the flour and beat hard until it forms a smooth ball that comes away from the sides. Leave to cool for 5 minutes.
2. Beat in the egg and vanilla until the dough is smooth and glossy. Spoon into a piping bag with a large star nozzle.
3. Mix the caster sugar and cinnamon on a plate.
4. Heat the cream until steaming, pour over the chocolate and stir until smooth.
5. Heat the oil in a large, deep pan to 180°C, or until a cube of bread browns in 30 seconds. Never fill the pan more than a third full. Pipe 10cm lengths into the oil, snipping them off with scissors, and fry a few at a time for 3–4 minutes until golden.
6. Drain on kitchen paper, roll in the cinnamon sugar while hot and serve with the chocolate sauce for dipping.

## Notes
- Without a piping bag, use a sandwich bag with the corner snipped off; they won't have ridges but taste the same.
- Best eaten straight away.
