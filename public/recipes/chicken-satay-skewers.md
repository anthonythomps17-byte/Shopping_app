# Chicken Satay Skewers
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, bbq, thai

Sticky marinated chicken skewers with a creamy peanut satay sauce, jasmine rice and a quick cucumber salad.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless, cut into strips
- 300g jasmine rice
- 1 cucumber, cut into ribbons
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 30g roasted peanuts, chopped

### For the marinade
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp medium curry powder
- 1 clove garlic, crushed

### For the satay sauce
- 4 tbsp crunchy peanut butter
- 200ml coconut milk
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 1 lime, juiced

## Method
1. Mix the marinade ingredients, add the chicken and leave for at least 20 minutes. Soak wooden skewers in water.
2. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
3. Put all the satay sauce ingredients in a small pan and warm gently, stirring, until smooth. Add a splash of water if it's too thick.
4. Thread the chicken onto the skewers and cook under a hot grill or on a griddle for 8–10 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked through.
5. Serve with the rice, cucumber and spring onions, with the satay sauce spooned over and the peanuts scattered on top.

## Notes
- Great on the barbecue.
