# Panzerotti
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 40 min, including proving
Tags: dinner, italian, street food, vegetarian, family, fakeaway

Little fried calzones from Puglia, stuffed with tomato and stringy mozzarella. Crisp outside, molten inside, and a fun one to make with kids.

## Ingredients
- 500g strong white bread flour
- 7g fast-action dried yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 300ml water, warm
- 200g passata
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 250g mozzarella, chopped
- 15g fresh basil
- 1 litre vegetable oil, for frying

### To serve
- 120g mixed salad leaves

## Method
1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt in a bowl. Add the olive oil and warm water and bring together into a dough. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and stretchy, then cover and leave somewhere warm for 1 hour, until doubled in size.
2. Mix the passata with the oregano and a pinch of salt. Pat the mozzarella dry with kitchen paper.
3. Split the dough into 12 and roll each piece out to a thin round about 12cm across.
4. Put a spoonful of passata, some mozzarella and a basil leaf on one half of each round, leaving a border. Fold over and press the edges firmly with a fork to seal.
5. Heat the oil in a large, deep pan to 170°C, or until a cube of bread browns in 40 seconds. Never fill the pan more than a third full. Fry 2 or 3 at a time for 2–3 minutes per side until puffed and golden. Drain on kitchen paper.
6. Leave to cool for a couple of minutes, as the filling is very hot, then serve with the salad.

## Notes
- To bake instead, brush with olive oil and bake at 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7 for 15–18 minutes.
- Add ham or pepperoni for meat eaters.
