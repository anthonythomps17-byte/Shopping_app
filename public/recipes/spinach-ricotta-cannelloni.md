# Spinach & Ricotta Cannelloni
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, vegetarian

Pasta tubes filled with creamy ricotta and spinach, baked in a garlicky tomato sauce under melted mozzarella.

## Ingredients
- 240g baby spinach
- 250g ricotta
- 40g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg
- 250g cannelloni tubes
- 125g mozzarella, torn
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 700g passata
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 15g fresh basil, torn

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. For the sauce, heat the oil and garlic in a pan for 1 minute, then add the passata and oregano and simmer for 10 minutes. Stir in the basil and season.
3. Wilt the spinach in a large pan with a splash of water. Cool, squeeze out as much water as you can, then chop.
4. Mix the spinach with the ricotta, half the hard cheese, the nutmeg and some seasoning. Pipe or spoon into the cannelloni tubes.
5. Spread half the sauce in a baking dish, lay the tubes on top and cover with the rest of the sauce. Top with the mozzarella and the rest of the hard cheese.
6. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the pasta is tender and the top is golden.

## Notes
- Or roll the filling up in fresh lasagne sheets.
