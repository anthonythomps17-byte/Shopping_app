# Beef & Broccoli Stir Fry
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, quick, chinese, fakeaway

Tender strips of steak and crunchy broccoli in a glossy garlic and oyster sauce. Faster than a takeaway.

## Ingredients
- 500g rump steak, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 300g basmati rice

### For the sauce
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp soft brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Toss the steak with the cornflour and a pinch of salt. Mix the sauce ingredients with 4 tbsp water.
3. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a wok over a very high heat. Stir fry the beef in two batches for 1–2 minutes until browned. Lift out.
4. Add the rest of the oil and the broccoli with a splash of water. Stir fry for 3 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger and most of the spring onions for 1 minute.
5. Return the beef, pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until glossy.
6. Serve on the rice, topped with the rest of the spring onions.

## Notes
- Slice the steak against the grain so it stays tender.
