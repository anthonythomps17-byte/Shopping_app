# Smoked Mackerel & Beetroot Salad
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, quick

Rich smoked mackerel with warm new potatoes, sweet beetroot and peppery rocket in a creamy horseradish dressing.

## Ingredients
- 500g new potatoes, halved
- 200g smoked mackerel fillets
- 250g cooked beetroot, cut into wedges
- 60g rocket
- 15g fresh chives, snipped

### For the dressing
- 3 tbsp crème fraîche
- 1 tbsp horseradish sauce
- ½ lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes until tender, then drain.
2. Mix the dressing ingredients with a little seasoning.
3. Flake the mackerel into large pieces, removing the skin.
4. Arrange the rocket, potatoes, beetroot and mackerel on plates, drizzle with the dressing and scatter with chives.

## Notes
- Smoked mackerel is cheap, keeps for weeks in the fridge and is full of omega-3.
