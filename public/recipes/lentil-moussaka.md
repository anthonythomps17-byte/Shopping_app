# Lentil Moussaka
Serves: 6
Time: 1 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, greek, family, comfort food, vegetarian

A cheaper, vegetarian moussaka with a rich lentil and tomato filling, roasted aubergine and a golden cheese sauce.

## Ingredients
- 3 aubergines, sliced into 1cm rounds
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tins green lentils (400g each), drained
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 200ml vegetable stock
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the topping
- 50g butter
- 50g plain flour
- 600ml milk
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg
- 1 egg, beaten
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Brush the aubergine slices with 3 tbsp of the oil, season, and roast on baking trays for 25 minutes until soft and golden.
2. Meanwhile, heat the rest of the oil in a large pan and cook the onion and carrots for 10 minutes. Add the garlic, cinnamon and oregano for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the tomato purée, lentils, tomatoes and stock and simmer for 15 minutes until thick. Season.
4. For the topping, melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the milk. Simmer for 5 minutes until thick. Season with salt and nutmeg, cool for a few minutes, then beat in the egg and half the cheese.
5. Layer half the aubergines in a baking dish, then the lentils, then the rest of the aubergines. Pour over the topping and scatter with the rest of the cheese.
6. Bake for 35–40 minutes until golden. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting.

## Notes
- Freezes well in portions.
