# Gammon, Egg & Chips
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, british, easy

A pub classic: gammon steaks with fried eggs, homemade chips and peas. Add pineapple if you like.

## Ingredients
- 4 gammon steaks
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), cut into chips
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 eggs
- 300g frozen peas
- 1 tin pineapple rings (227g), drained, optional
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7. Toss the chips with 2 tbsp of the oil and some salt, spread out on a large baking tray and roast for 40 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.
2. Snip the fat on the gammon every couple of centimetres so it doesn't curl. Cook under a hot grill or in a griddle pan for 4–5 minutes per side. Add the pineapple to the pan for the last 2 minutes, if using.
3. Fry the eggs in the rest of the oil.
4. Cook the peas for 3 minutes. Serve the gammon topped with an egg, with the chips and peas.

## Notes
- Gammon can be salty, so you won't need to season it.
