# Bubble & Squeak with Fried Eggs
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: brunch, dinner, british, easy, vegetarian

Crispy fried patties of mashed potato and cabbage, topped with fried eggs. Brilliant for using up leftovers.

## Ingredients
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- ½ savoy cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 25g butter
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 eggs
- 1 tin baked beans (415g)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes until tender, adding the cabbage for the last 4 minutes. Drain well and mash roughly.
2. Fry the onion in the butter for 8 minutes until soft, then mix into the mash and season.
3. Shape into 8 patties. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large frying pan and fry the patties for 4–5 minutes per side until crisp and golden.
4. Meanwhile, warm the beans and fry the eggs in the rest of the oil.
5. Serve the bubble and squeak topped with the eggs, with the beans.

## Notes
- Use leftover mash and any cooked greens, sprouts or carrots.
