# Peri-Peri Chicken & Spicy Rice
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, fakeaway, bbq

Charred, spicy chicken thighs with a peppery tomato rice, like your favourite chicken restaurant.

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 4 tbsp peri-peri sauce
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 300g long grain rice
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 700ml chicken stock
- 1 tin sweetcorn (198g), drained
- 100g frozen peas

## Method
1. Mix the chicken with the peri-peri sauce and lemon juice and leave for 15 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
2. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onion and pepper for 6 minutes. Add the garlic, paprika and turmeric for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the rice and tomato purée, then pour in the stock. Cover and simmer gently for 15 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, heat a griddle pan or grill to high and cook the chicken for 6–7 minutes per side until charred and cooked through.
5. Stir the sweetcorn and peas into the rice and cook for 3 minutes more, until the rice is tender.
6. Serve the chicken on the rice, with extra peri-peri sauce on the side.

## Notes
- Great on the barbecue in summer.
