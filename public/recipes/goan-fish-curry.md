# Goan Fish Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, curry

Chunks of white fish simmered in a tangy, spiced coconut and tomato sauce. Light and full of flavour.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, sliced
- 2 tsp medium curry powder
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 4 white fish fillets (cod, haddock or pollock), cut into chunks
- 1 lime, juiced
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 300g basmati rice

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions.
2. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, chilli and spices for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and coconut milk and simmer for 10 minutes until slightly thickened.
4. Add the fish and simmer gently for 5–6 minutes until it flakes easily.
5. Stir in the lime juice, scatter with coriander and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Add a handful of prawns with the fish for a seafood version.
