# Tomato & Red Lentil Soup
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: lunch, soup, batch cook, vegetarian, vegan

A thick, warming soup that's cheap to make, packed with protein, and freezes brilliantly.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 sticks celery, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 150g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onion, carrots and celery for 10 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
2. Add the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 20–25 minutes until the lentils are soft.
3. Blend until smooth (or leave chunky), season and serve with crusty bread.

## Notes
- Freezes for up to 3 months.
- Swirl in some yoghurt or a pinch of chilli flakes to serve.
