# Ratatouille
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, one pan, batch cook, vegetarian, vegan

A slow-cooked Provençal stew of aubergine, courgettes, peppers and tomatoes. Simple, cheap and full of summer flavour.

## Ingredients
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1 aubergine, cut into chunks
- 2 courgettes, cut into chunks
- 1 red pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 yellow pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 15g fresh basil
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a large casserole and fry the aubergine for 8 minutes until golden. Lift out.
2. Add the rest of the oil, then fry the courgettes and peppers for 5 minutes. Lift out.
3. Cook the onion for 8 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute. Stir in the tomatoes, purée and thyme.
4. Return all the vegetables, cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes, stirring now and then. Season and stir in the basil.
5. Serve with crusty bread, or with rice, couscous or pasta.

## Notes
- Freezes well. Even better the next day.
