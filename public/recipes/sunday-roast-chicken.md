# Sunday Roast Chicken
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 45 min
Tags: dinner, sunday roast, british

A classic roast chicken with lemon and garlic, crispy roast potatoes, carrots and proper gravy.

## Ingredients
- 1 whole chicken (about 1.6kg)
- 1 lemon, halved
- 1 head garlic, halved across the middle
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 25g butter, softened
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and cut into chunks
- 4 tbsp vegetable oil
- 500g carrots, cut into batons
- 200g frozen peas
- 2 tbsp gravy granules
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before.
2. Stuff the chicken with the lemon, garlic and thyme. Rub the butter over the skin and season. Roast for 1 hr 20 min, or until the juices run clear when the thickest part of the thigh is pierced.
3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 8 minutes, then drain and shake to rough up the edges. Heat the oil in a roasting tin in the oven for 5 minutes, add the potatoes, turn to coat, and roast for 50–60 minutes, turning halfway.
4. Rest the chicken under foil for 20 minutes. Meanwhile, boil the carrots for 8 minutes and the peas for 3 minutes.
5. Make the gravy with the granules, the chicken's resting juices and some of the vegetable water.
6. Carve the chicken and serve with the potatoes, vegetables and gravy.

## Notes
- Use the carcass to make stock for the chicken noodle soup.
