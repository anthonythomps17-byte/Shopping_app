# Pasta Puttanesca
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, quick, vegetarian

A punchy store-cupboard pasta with tomatoes, olives, capers, garlic and chilli. Ready in 20 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 350g spaghetti
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 75g black olives, halved
- 2 tbsp capers
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 30g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the spaghetti following the pack instructions.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the garlic and chilli for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, olives and capers and simmer for 10 minutes until thick. Season.
4. Drain the pasta and toss with the sauce and most of the parsley. Serve with the cheese and the rest of the parsley.

## Notes
- Traditionally it has anchovies: add a few, melted into the oil with the garlic, if you're not cooking veggie.
