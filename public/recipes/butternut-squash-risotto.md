# Butternut Squash Risotto
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, italian, comfort food, vegetarian

Creamy risotto with sweet roasted butternut squash and crispy sage.

## Ingredients
- 1 butternut squash, peeled and cut into 2cm chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 25g butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 300g risotto rice
- 150ml white wine, optional
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock, hot
- 50g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- 15g fresh sage
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Toss the squash with 1 tbsp of the oil and some seasoning and roast for 30 minutes until soft and golden.
2. Meanwhile, melt half the butter in a large pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes. Add the garlic and rice and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the wine, if using, and stir until absorbed. Add the stock a ladleful at a time, stirring and letting each one be absorbed before adding the next, for about 20 minutes until the rice is creamy and just tender.
4. Fry the sage leaves in the rest of the oil for 1 minute until crisp.
5. Mash half the squash into the risotto, then stir in the rest with the cheese and remaining butter. Season and serve topped with the crispy sage.

## Notes
- Use extra stock instead of the wine if you like.
