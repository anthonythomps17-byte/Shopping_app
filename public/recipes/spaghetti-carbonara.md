# Spaghetti Carbonara
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, italian, quick

The real thing: silky egg, parmesan and crispy pancetta, with no cream. On the table in 20 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 400g spaghetti
- 150g pancetta, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, peeled and lightly crushed
- 3 eggs
- 2 egg yolks
- 60g parmesan, finely grated, plus extra to serve
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the spaghetti in a large pan of salted boiling water following the pack instructions.
2. Meanwhile, beat the eggs, yolks and parmesan with plenty of black pepper.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and fry the pancetta and garlic for 5 minutes until the pancetta is crisp. Throw away the garlic.
4. Save a mugful of the pasta water, then drain the spaghetti and toss it in the pan with the pancetta. Take the pan off the heat.
5. Pour in the egg mixture with a splash of pasta water and toss quickly until creamy, adding more water if needed. Serve straight away with extra parmesan.

## Notes
- Take the pan off the heat before adding the eggs, so they don't scramble.
- Smoked bacon lardons work if you can't find pancetta.
