# Korean Beef Bowls
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: dinner, quick, fakeaway

Sweet, spicy and savoury beef mince with rice, crunchy cucumber and a fried egg. A 25-minute dinner.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 500g beef mince
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 300g basmati rice
- 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- 4 eggs
- 4 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

### For the sauce
- 2 tbsp gochujang paste
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar

## Method
1. Cook the rice following the pack instructions. Mix the sauce ingredients together.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a high heat and fry the mince, breaking it up, until browned and a little crispy.
3. Add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then pour in the sauce and bubble for 2–3 minutes until sticky.
4. Meanwhile, fry the eggs to your liking.
5. Divide the rice between bowls and top with the beef, cucumber, carrot and a fried egg. Scatter with spring onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Gochujang is a Korean chilli paste. Find it in the world foods aisle, or use sriracha.
