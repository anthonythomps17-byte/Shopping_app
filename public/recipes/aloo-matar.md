# Aloo Matar (Potato & Pea Curry)
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: dinner, curry, easy, vegetarian, vegan

A simple, comforting Indian curry of potatoes and peas in a spiced tomato sauce. Costs very little to make.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 tsp medium curry powder
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 750g potatoes, peeled and cut into 2cm cubes
- 300g frozen peas
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 300g basmati rice

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and add the cumin seeds. When they sizzle, add the onion and cook for 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, ginger, turmeric and curry powder and cook for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, potatoes and 300ml water. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
4. Meanwhile, cook the rice following the pack instructions.
5. Add the peas and garam masala to the curry and cook for 3 minutes. Scatter with coriander and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Add a handful of spinach at the end for extra greens.
