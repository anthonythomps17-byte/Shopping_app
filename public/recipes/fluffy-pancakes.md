# Fluffy American Pancakes
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: breakfast, brunch, family, vegetarian

Thick, fluffy pancakes stacked high with blueberries, yoghurt and maple syrup. A weekend favourite.

## Ingredients
- 200g self-raising flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 eggs
- 250ml milk
- 25g butter, melted, plus extra for frying
- 150g blueberries
- 150g Greek yoghurt
- 4 tbsp maple syrup

## Method
1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and a pinch of salt in a bowl.
2. Whisk the eggs, milk and melted butter, then whisk into the flour to make a thick, smooth batter.
3. Heat a little butter in a large non-stick frying pan over a medium heat. Add ladlefuls of batter and cook for 2 minutes until bubbles appear on top, then flip and cook for 1–2 minutes more.
4. Keep warm while you cook the rest, then stack up and serve with the blueberries, yoghurt and maple syrup.

## Notes
- Stir some of the blueberries into the batter for blueberry pancakes.
