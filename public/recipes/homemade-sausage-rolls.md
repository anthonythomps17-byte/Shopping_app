# Homemade Sausage Rolls
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: lunch, british, family, easy

Flaky, golden sausage rolls that are cheaper and tastier than shop-bought. Great for lunchboxes and picnics.

## Ingredients
- 400g sausage meat
- 1 onion, very finely chopped
- 1 apple, grated
- 1 tsp dried sage
- 320g puff pastry, ready rolled
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tin baked beans (415g)
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6.
2. Mix the sausage meat with the onion, apple, sage and plenty of black pepper.
3. Cut the pastry in half lengthways. Lay a sausage of the filling down the middle of each strip, brush one edge with egg, fold over and press to seal.
4. Cut each into 6 rolls, put on a lined baking tray, brush with egg and scatter with sesame seeds. Cut two small slits in each.
5. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and cooked through. Serve warm with beans, or cold.

## Notes
- Freeze uncooked rolls on a tray, then bag up. Bake from frozen for 35 minutes.
