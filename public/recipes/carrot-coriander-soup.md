# Carrot & Coriander Soup
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: lunch, soup, easy, vegetarian, vegan

A smooth, sweet and fragrant soup made from a bag of carrots. One of the cheapest meals there is.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 potato, peeled and chopped
- 1kg carrots, chopped
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 6 minutes until soft.
2. Add the potato, carrots and ground coriander and stir for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 20 minutes until the carrots are soft.
4. Blend until smooth, stir in most of the fresh coriander and season. Serve topped with the rest, with crusty bread.

## Notes
- Freezes brilliantly.
