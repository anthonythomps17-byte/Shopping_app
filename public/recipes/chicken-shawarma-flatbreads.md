# Chicken Shawarma Flatbreads
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, middle eastern, quick

Warmly spiced chicken with garlicky yoghurt, hummus and crunchy salad, all piled into warm flatbreads.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 4 flatbreads
- 200g hummus
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- ½ cucumber, chopped
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced

### For the garlic yoghurt
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 1 clove garlic, crushed

## Method
1. Mix the oil, lemon juice, garlic and spices, then rub into the chicken. Leave for at least 15 minutes.
2. Heat a griddle or frying pan over a high heat and cook the chicken for 5–6 minutes per side until charred and cooked through. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
3. Mix the yoghurt with the garlic and a pinch of salt.
4. Warm the flatbreads, spread with hummus, then top with the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, onion, chicken and garlic yoghurt.

## Notes
- Pittas or tortilla wraps work too.
