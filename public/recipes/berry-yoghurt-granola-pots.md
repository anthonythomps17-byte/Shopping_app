# Berry Yoghurt & Granola Pots
Serves: 2
Time: 5 min
Tags: breakfast, quick, easy, vegetarian

Layers of creamy yoghurt, crunchy granola and fresh berries. A no-cook breakfast in five minutes.

## Ingredients
- 300g Greek yoghurt
- 80g granola
- 150g raspberries
- 150g blueberries
- 2 tbsp honey

## Method
1. Spoon a layer of yoghurt into two glasses or jars.
2. Add a layer of berries, then granola. Repeat.
3. Drizzle with honey and serve straight away, or add the granola just before eating if you're taking it with you.

## Notes
- Frozen berries work well: defrost them in the fridge overnight.
