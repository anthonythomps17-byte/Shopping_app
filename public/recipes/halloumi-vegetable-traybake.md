# Halloumi & Vegetable Traybake
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: dinner, traybake, easy, vegetarian

Golden halloumi roasted with peppers, courgette, red onion and chickpeas, finished with lemon and mint.

## Ingredients
- 2 red peppers, cut into chunks
- 1 yellow pepper, cut into chunks
- 2 courgettes, cut into chunks
- 2 red onions, cut into wedges
- 1 tin chickpeas (400g), drained
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 225g halloumi, sliced
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- 150g Greek yoghurt
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7.
2. Toss the peppers, courgettes, onions and chickpeas with the oil, spices and seasoning in a large roasting tin. Roast for 20 minutes.
3. Add the tomatoes and lay the halloumi slices on top. Roast for 15 minutes more, until the halloumi is golden.
4. Squeeze over the lemon, scatter with mint and serve with the yoghurt.

## Notes
- Serve with couscous or warm flatbreads to make it go further.
