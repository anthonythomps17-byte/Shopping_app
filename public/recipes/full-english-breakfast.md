# Full English Breakfast
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: brunch, british

The works: sausages, bacon, eggs, beans, mushrooms, tomatoes, black pudding and toast. The weekend sorted.

## Ingredients
- 8 pork sausages
- 8 rashers smoked back bacon
- 4 slices black pudding
- 4 tomatoes, halved
- 250g chestnut mushrooms, thickly sliced
- 1 tin baked beans (415g)
- 4 eggs
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 15g butter
- 8 slices bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Roast the sausages on a tray for 25 minutes, turning halfway. Add the bacon, black pudding and tomatoes for the last 12 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, fry the mushrooms in the butter for 6–8 minutes until golden. Season.
3. Warm the beans in a small pan.
4. Fry the eggs in the oil and toast the bread.
5. Pile everything onto warm plates and serve straight away.

## Notes
- Swap in hash browns or a slice of fried bread if you like.
