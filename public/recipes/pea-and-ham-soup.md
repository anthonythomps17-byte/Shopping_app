# Pea & Ham Soup
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: lunch, soup, british, quick, easy

A bright green, thick soup made with frozen peas and chunks of ham. Cheap, quick and filling.

## Ingredients
- 25g butter
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 potato, peeled and diced
- 900g frozen peas
- 1 litre chicken stock
- 200g cooked ham, chopped
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- 4 slices crusty bread
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the onion for 6 minutes until soft.
2. Add the potato and stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 10 minutes until the potato is tender.
3. Add the peas and simmer for 5 minutes. Stir in the mint, then blend until smooth.
4. Stir in most of the ham, season, and serve topped with the rest of the ham, with crusty bread.

## Notes
- Freezes well. Use vegetable stock and leave out the ham for a veggie version.
