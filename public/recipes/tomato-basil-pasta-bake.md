# Tomato & Mozzarella Pasta Bake
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, family, easy, vegetarian

Pasta in a simple tomato and basil sauce, baked under bubbling mozzarella and cheddar. Kids love it.

## Ingredients
- 350g penne
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 500g passata
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 15g fresh basil
- 125g mozzarella, torn
- 50g cheddar cheese, grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Cook the penne for 2 minutes less than the pack says, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan and cook the onion for 6 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute, then the tomatoes, passata and oregano. Simmer for 10 minutes, then tear in the basil and season.
3. Mix the pasta with the sauce, tip into a baking dish and top with the mozzarella and cheddar.
4. Bake for 20 minutes until golden and bubbling.

## Notes
- Hide extra veg in the sauce: grated carrot or courgette, or a handful of spinach.
