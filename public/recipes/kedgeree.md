# Kedgeree
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min
Tags: brunch, dinner, british

Smoked haddock, gently spiced rice and soft-boiled eggs. A classic brunch that makes a great dinner too.

## Ingredients
- 400g smoked haddock fillets
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 eggs
- 25g butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tsp medium curry powder
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 300g basmati rice
- 100g frozen peas
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Put the haddock and bay leaves in a frying pan, cover with 600ml water and simmer gently for 5 minutes. Lift out the fish and save the water.
2. Boil the eggs for 7 minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
3. Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes until soft. Stir in the curry powder, turmeric and rice for 1 minute.
4. Pour in the fish water, cover and simmer for 12 minutes. Add the peas for the last 3 minutes.
5. Flake the haddock into the rice, removing any skin and bones. Fold in the parsley and season.
6. Serve topped with the eggs, halved, and lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Choose undyed smoked haddock if you can find it.
