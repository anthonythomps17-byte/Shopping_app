# Mushroom Bourguignon
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, comfort food, vegetarian

A rich, wintry French stew of mushrooms, shallots and carrots in red wine, served with creamy mash.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 25g butter
- 500g chestnut mushrooms, halved
- 250g portobello mushrooms, cut into chunks
- 8 shallots, peeled and halved
- 2 carrots, cut into chunks
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 300ml red wine
- 300ml vegetable stock
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and chopped
- 75ml milk
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil and half the butter in a large casserole. Fry the mushrooms in batches for 5 minutes until golden. Lift out.
2. Add the shallots and carrots and cook for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, tomato purée and flour and stir for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the wine and bubble for 2 minutes, then add the stock, thyme and bay leaves. Return the mushrooms and simmer for 30 minutes until thick and the carrots are tender. Season.
4. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes, then mash with the rest of the butter and the milk.
5. Serve the bourguignon on the mash, scattered with parsley.

## Notes
- Use extra stock and a splash of balsamic vinegar instead of the wine if you prefer.
