# Turkish Mackerel Sandwiches
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, turkish, street food, quick

Balık ekmek, the fish sandwich sold from boats on the Bosphorus: crisp grilled mackerel in a crusty roll with lettuce, onion, tomato and a squeeze of lemon.

## Ingredients
- 4 mackerel fillets
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 4 crusty white rolls
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ground sumac
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the grill to high. Rub the mackerel with the oil, paprika, oregano and some salt and pepper, and lay skin-side up on a lined tray.
2. Toss the onion with the sumac, parsley and a pinch of salt.
3. Grill the mackerel for 4–5 minutes until the skin is blistered and the fish is just cooked.
4. Split the rolls and warm them briefly under the grill.
5. Fill each roll with lettuce, tomato, a mackerel fillet and the onion salad. Finish with a good squeeze of lemon and a pinch of chilli flakes.

## Notes
- Fresh mackerel is cheap and full of omega-3. Ask the fish counter to fillet it if it's whole.
- Sardine or sea bass fillets work too.
