# Lamb Kofta with Tzatziki
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, middle eastern, bbq, quick

Spiced lamb skewers with cool, minty tzatziki, warm pittas and a crunchy salad.

## Ingredients
- 500g lamb mince
- 1 onion, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- 4 pittas
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the tzatziki
- 200g Greek yoghurt
- ½ cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- ½ lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Mix the lamb, onion, garlic, spices, half the mint and plenty of seasoning. Shape into 8 sausage shapes around skewers (soak wooden ones in water first).
2. Mix the tzatziki ingredients with the rest of the mint and a pinch of salt.
3. Cook the kofta on a hot griddle, grill or barbecue for 8–10 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
4. Warm the pittas and serve with the kofta, salad and tzatziki.

## Notes
- Also good with couscous or rice instead of pittas.
