# Falafel Wraps
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: lunch, middle eastern, vegetarian

Crispy homemade chickpea falafel wrapped up with hummus, salad and a garlicky yoghurt sauce.

## Ingredients
- 2 tins chickpeas (400g each), drained and dried
- 1 red onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 3 tbsp plain flour
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 flour tortillas (large)
- 200g hummus
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- ½ cucumber, chopped
- 150g Greek yoghurt
- ½ lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Blitz the chickpeas, half the onion, the garlic, spices and parsley in a food processor until finely chopped but not smooth. Stir in the flour and season. Shape into 16 small patties.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and fry the falafel for 3 minutes per side until golden and crisp.
3. Mix the yoghurt with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
4. Spread the tortillas with hummus, then fill with lettuce, tomato, cucumber, the rest of the onion and the falafel. Drizzle with the yoghurt and roll up.

## Notes
- Use dairy-free yoghurt to make them vegan.
