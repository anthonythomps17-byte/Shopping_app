# Fish Finger Sandwiches
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min
Tags: lunch, dinner, family, quick, easy

Crispy fish fingers in soft rolls with lettuce and tartare sauce, with minty smashed peas on the side. Pure comfort.

## Ingredients
- 12 fish fingers
- 4 soft rolls
- 15g butter
- 1 little gem lettuce, shredded
- 4 tbsp tartare sauce
- 300g frozen peas
- 15g fresh mint, chopped
- ½ lemon, juiced
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Cook the fish fingers following the pack instructions.
2. Cook the peas for 3 minutes, then drain and roughly crush with the butter, mint, lemon juice and seasoning.
3. Split the rolls and fill with lettuce, fish fingers and tartare sauce. Serve with the smashed peas.

## Notes
- Ketchup works just as well as tartare sauce for the kids.
