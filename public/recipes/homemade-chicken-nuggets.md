# Homemade Chicken Nuggets
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, family, easy

Crispy oven-baked chicken nuggets that the kids can help make. Serve with oven chips, peas and ketchup.

## Ingredients
- 3 chicken breasts (about 450g), cut into chunks
- 50g plain flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 100g breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 750g frozen oven chips
- 300g frozen peas
- 4 tbsp tomato ketchup
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan), gas 7. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
2. Mix the breadcrumbs with the paprika and oil. Season the flour.
3. Toss the chicken in the flour, dip in the egg, then press into the breadcrumbs.
4. Spread out on a lined baking tray and bake for 15–18 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and cooked through.
5. Cook the peas for 3 minutes and serve with the nuggets, chips and ketchup.

## Notes
- Crushed cornflakes make an extra-crunchy coating.
- Freeze uncooked nuggets on a tray, then bag up. Cook from frozen for 20–25 minutes.
