# Prego Steak Rolls
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, lunch, street food, steak, quick

Portugal's favourite snack-bar sandwich: thin beef steaks marinated in garlic, bay and white wine, seared fast and stuffed into crusty rolls with mustard and a dash of piri-piri.

## Ingredients
- 500g thin-cut beef steaks (4 minute steaks)
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 bay leaves, torn
- 100ml white wine
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 15g butter
- 4 crusty white rolls
- 1 tbsp English mustard
- 2 tbsp peri-peri sauce
- 60g rocket
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 750g frozen oven chips

## Method
1. Season the steaks well with salt and pepper and put in a dish with the garlic, bay, wine and paprika. Leave for 15 minutes, or a few hours in the fridge.
2. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a very high heat. Lift the steaks out of the marinade and sear for 1 minute per side. Lift onto a plate.
4. Pour the marinade into the pan with the butter and bubble for 1–2 minutes until slightly reduced. Return the steaks and turn them in the sauce.
5. Split and warm the rolls, spread with mustard and dip the cut sides in the pan juices. Fill with the steaks, a splash of peri-peri sauce and some rocket, and serve with the chips.

## Notes
- Top each steak with a fried egg for a prego no prato.
