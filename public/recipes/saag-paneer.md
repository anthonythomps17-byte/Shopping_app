# Saag Paneer
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, curry, vegetarian

Golden cubes of paneer in a spiced, creamy spinach sauce. Rich and comforting.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 450g paneer, cubed
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 480g baby spinach
- 100ml double cream
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 300g basmati rice
- Salt, to taste

## Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp of the oil in a large frying pan and fry the paneer for 5 minutes until golden on all sides. Lift out.
2. Add the rest of the oil and the cumin seeds. When they sizzle, add the onion and cook for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, chilli, turmeric and garam masala for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook for 3 minutes, then add the spinach a handful at a time until wilted.
4. Blend half the spinach mixture until smooth, then stir it back in with the cream and lemon juice. Return the paneer and heat through. Season.
5. Serve with rice, or with naan.

## Notes
- Use frozen spinach to save money: defrost and squeeze it dry first.
