# Minestrone Soup
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min
Tags: lunch, soup, italian, vegetarian

A hearty Italian vegetable soup with beans and pasta. A meal in a bowl.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 sticks celery, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 courgette, diced
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock
- 1 tin cannellini beans (400g), drained
- 75g small pasta shapes
- 100g kale, shredded
- 2 tbsp green pesto, vegetarian
- 30g vegetarian hard cheese, finely grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion, carrots and celery for 10 minutes. Add the garlic and courgette for 2 minutes.
2. Add the tomatoes and stock and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Add the beans and pasta and cook for 8–10 minutes until the pasta is tender. Stir in the kale for the last 3 minutes.
4. Season and serve topped with a spoonful of pesto and the cheese.

## Notes
- Use any small pasta, or broken-up spaghetti.
