# Chicken Kiev
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, british, family

Crispy breaded chicken breasts with a molten garlic and herb butter centre. Much better than the frozen ones.

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts (about 600g)
- 75g butter, softened
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 15g fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 50g plain flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 100g panko breadcrumbs
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 750g new potatoes
- 200g green beans
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Mash the butter with the garlic, parsley and a pinch of salt. Chill until firm.
2. Cut a deep pocket into the side of each chicken breast, without cutting all the way through. Stuff with the garlic butter and press the edges closed.
3. Dust the chicken in the flour, dip in the egg, then coat in the panko. Dip in the egg and panko again for an extra-crisp coating. Chill for 15 minutes.
4. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6. Heat the oil in a frying pan and brown the kievs for 2 minutes per side, then bake on a tray for 20–25 minutes until cooked through.
5. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 15–20 minutes, adding the green beans for the last 4 minutes. Serve with the kievs.

## Notes
- The double coating helps keep the butter inside.
