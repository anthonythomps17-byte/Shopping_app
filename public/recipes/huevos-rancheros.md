# Huevos Rancheros
Serves: 4
Time: 25 min
Tags: breakfast, brunch, mexican, vegetarian

Fried eggs on crispy tortillas with a spicy tomato and black bean sauce, avocado and feta. A Mexican-style brunch.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin black beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 4 flour tortillas (small)
- 4 eggs
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 50g feta, crumbled
- 15g fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat half the oil in a frying pan and cook the onion for 6 minutes. Add the garlic, cumin and chilli for 1 minute.
2. Add the tomatoes and beans and simmer for 8 minutes until thick. Season.
3. Meanwhile, toast the tortillas in a dry frying pan until crisp, and fry the eggs in the rest of the oil.
4. Spoon the beans onto the tortillas and top with the eggs, avocado, feta and coriander. Serve with lime wedges.

## Notes
- Swap the feta for grated cheddar if you prefer.
