# Lancashire Hotpot
Serves: 4
Time: 2 hr 30 min
Tags: dinner, british, comfort food, slow cook

Tender lamb and onions slowly baked under a crispy layer of sliced potatoes. A Northern classic.

## Ingredients
- 800g diced lamb shoulder
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 onions, sliced
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 500ml lamb stock
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1kg potatoes (floury, like Maris Piper), peeled and thinly sliced
- 25g butter, melted
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 160°C (140°C fan), gas 3. Toss the lamb in the flour with some seasoning.
2. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof casserole and brown the lamb in batches. Lift out.
3. Add the onions and carrots and cook for 8 minutes. Return the lamb with the stock, Worcestershire sauce, thyme and bay leaves.
4. Arrange the potato slices over the top, overlapping like roof tiles. Brush with the melted butter and season.
5. Cover and bake for 1 hr 30 min, then uncover, turn the oven up to 200°C (180°C fan), gas 6, and bake for 30 minutes more until the potatoes are golden and crisp.

## Notes
- Serve with pickled red cabbage, the traditional way.
