# Kibbeling with Garlic Sauce
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, street food, fakeaway

Dutch market-stall fish bites: chunks of white fish in a light, spiced batter, fried until crisp and dunked in a garlicky herb sauce. Served here with oven chips.

## Ingredients
- 600g white fish fillets (cod or pollock), cut into bite-size chunks
- 100g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 175ml sparkling water, ice-cold
- 1 litre vegetable oil, for frying
- 750g frozen oven chips
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the garlic sauce
- 4 tbsp mayonnaise
- 4 tbsp Greek yoghurt
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 15g fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tsp lemon juice

## Method
1. Cook the chips following the pack instructions.
2. Mix together the garlic sauce ingredients with a pinch of salt.
3. Whisk the flour, baking powder, curry powder, paprika and 1 tsp salt with the sparkling water until just smooth, to make a thick batter.
4. Heat the oil in a large, deep pan to 180°C, or until a cube of bread browns in 30 seconds. Never fill the pan more than a third full.
5. Pat the fish dry, dip a few pieces at a time in the batter and fry for 3–4 minutes until crisp and golden. Drain on kitchen paper and sprinkle with salt.
6. Serve with the chips, garlic sauce and lemon wedges.

## Notes
- Any firm white fish works, including frozen fillets, defrosted and patted very dry.
