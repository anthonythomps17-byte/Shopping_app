# Steak Fajitas
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, steak, mexican, quick

Spiced rump steak strips with charred peppers and onions, wrapped in warm tortillas.

## Ingredients
- 600g rump steak, trimmed
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 1 green pepper, sliced
- 1 yellow pepper, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 8 flour tortillas
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

### For the marinade
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp garlic granules
- 1 lime, juiced
- Salt, to taste

### To serve
- 150ml soured cream
- 1 jar salsa (about 300g)
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 100g cheddar cheese, grated

## Method
1. Mix the paprika, cumin, chilli powder, garlic granules, lime juice, 1 tbsp of the oil and a pinch of salt. Rub over the steak and leave for 15 minutes.
2. Heat a large frying pan or griddle over a high heat. Cook the steak for 2–3 minutes per side for medium-rare, then rest for 5 minutes.
3. Add the rest of the oil to the pan and cook the peppers and onion for 6–8 minutes until softened and charred at the edges.
4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan or the microwave.
5. Slice the steak thinly against the grain and toss with the peppers and any resting juices.
6. Serve with the tortillas, soured cream, salsa, avocado, cheese and lime wedges.

## Notes
- Sirloin or skirt steak also work.
- Swap the tortillas for rice to make a burrito bowl.
