# Thai Green Chicken Curry
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min
Tags: dinner, curry, quick

A fragrant, creamy coconut curry with chicken, green beans and peppers.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken thigh fillets, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp Thai green curry paste
- 1 tin coconut milk (400ml)
- 150ml chicken stock
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 150g green beans, halved
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 bunch fresh coriander, chopped
- 300g jasmine rice

## Method
1. Rinse the rice and cook it following the pack instructions.
2. Heat the oil in a large pan or wok over a medium-high heat. Fry the curry paste for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken and stir to coat, cooking for 3–4 minutes.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and stock and bring to a simmer. Add the pepper and green beans and simmer for 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
5. Stir in the fish sauce, sugar and lime juice. Taste and add more fish sauce or lime if needed.
6. Scatter over the coriander and serve with the rice.

## Notes
- Use more or less curry paste depending on how hot you like it.
- Prawns work too: add them for the last 3 minutes only.
