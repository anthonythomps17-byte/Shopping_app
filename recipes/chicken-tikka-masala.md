# Chicken Tikka Masala
Serves: 4
Time: 45 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, curry, fakeaway

Yoghurt-marinated chicken in a creamy, mildly spiced tomato sauce. Better than the takeaway.

## Ingredients
- 600g chicken breast, cut into chunks
- 300g basmati rice
- 4 naan breads
- 1 bunch fresh coriander, chopped

### For the marinade
- 150g natural yoghurt
- 2 tbsp tikka masala paste
- 1 lemon, juiced

### For the sauce
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 20g fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp tikka masala paste
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 150ml double cream
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt, to taste

## Method
1. Mix the yoghurt, 2 tbsp curry paste and lemon juice. Stir in the chicken and leave for at least 30 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
2. Heat the grill to high. Thread the chicken onto skewers or spread it on a lined tray and grill for 10–12 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.
3. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes until soft and golden. Add the garlic, ginger, curry paste and garam masala and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes. Blitz with a stick blender until smooth, if you like.
5. Stir in the cream, sugar and grilled chicken and simmer for 5 minutes. Season to taste.
6. Cook the rice following the pack instructions and warm the naan. Serve topped with coriander.

## Notes
- Chicken thighs stay juicier if you have them.
- Use single cream or coconut milk for a lighter sauce.
