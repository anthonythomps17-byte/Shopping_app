# Honey Garlic Salmon Traybake
Serves: 4
Time: 35 min
Tags: dinner, fish, traybake, easy

Salmon fillets roasted on one tray with new potatoes, tenderstem and cherry tomatoes, glazed with honey, garlic and soy.

## Ingredients
- 4 salmon fillets (about 120g each)
- 750g baby new potatoes, halved
- 200g tenderstem broccoli
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the glaze
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 lemon, juiced

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan). Toss the potatoes in 1 tbsp of the oil, season and roast on a large tray for 20 minutes.
2. Mix the honey, soy sauce, garlic and lemon juice in a small bowl.
3. Push the potatoes to the sides. Add the broccoli and tomatoes, drizzle with the rest of the oil and nestle the salmon in the middle.
4. Spoon the glaze over the salmon and roast for 12–15 minutes until the salmon flakes easily.
5. Spoon any juices from the tray over everything and serve.

## Notes
- Line the tray with baking paper, as the glaze gets sticky.
- Swap the salmon for chicken breasts and roast for 20–25 minutes instead.
