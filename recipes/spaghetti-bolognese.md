# Spaghetti Bolognese
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, pasta, family

A rich, slow-simmered beef ragù with red wine and herbs, served over spaghetti.

## Ingredients
- 500g beef mince (5% fat)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 1 celery stick, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (400g each)
- 150ml red wine (optional)
- 200ml beef stock
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp sugar
- 400g spaghetti
- 30g parmesan, grated
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Cook the onion, carrot and celery for 8–10 minutes until soft.
2. Turn the heat up, add the mince and cook for 5 minutes, breaking it up, until browned all over. Stir in the garlic and tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the wine (if using) and let it bubble for 2 minutes. Add the tomatoes, stock, oregano and sugar.
4. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 30 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. Season to taste.
5. Cook the spaghetti following the pack instructions. Drain, keeping a mug of the cooking water.
6. Toss the spaghetti with the sauce, loosening with a splash of pasta water, and serve topped with parmesan.

## Notes
- Freezes well for up to 3 months, so it's worth doubling.
- Swap the wine for extra stock.
