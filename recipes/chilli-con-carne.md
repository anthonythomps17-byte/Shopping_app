# Chilli con Carne
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr
Tags: dinner, mexican, batch cook

A smoky beef and bean chilli, served with rice, soured cream and cheese.

## Ingredients
- 500g beef mince
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp chilli powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin kidney beans (400g), drained and rinsed
- 300ml beef stock
- 10g dark chocolate (optional)
- 300g long grain rice
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### To serve
- 150ml soured cream
- 75g cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 bunch fresh coriander, chopped

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large pan over a medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper for 6–8 minutes until softened.
2. Add the mince and cook for 5 minutes until browned, breaking it up as you go.
3. Stir in the garlic, chilli powder, cumin, paprika and oregano and cook for 1 minute, then add the tomato purée.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes, kidney beans and stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer gently for 35–40 minutes until thick, stirring now and then.
5. Stir in the chocolate, if using, and season to taste.
6. Cook the rice following the pack instructions. Serve the chilli with rice, soured cream, cheese and coriander.

## Notes
- Tastes even better the next day. Freezes for up to 3 months.
- Leftovers make great jacket potato toppings.
