# Chimichurri Flank Steak
Serves: 4
Time: 30 min, plus marinating
Tags: dinner, steak, bbq

Flank steak seared hot and sliced thin, topped with a fresh, garlicky Argentinian herb sauce.

## Ingredients
- 800g flank steak
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 750g new potatoes, halved
- 1 bag mixed salad leaves (about 120g)

### For the chimichurri
- 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley (about 30g), finely chopped
- 1 bunch fresh coriander (about 15g), finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 red chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 120ml extra virgin olive oil

## Method
1. Mix all the chimichurri ingredients with a good pinch of salt. Leave for at least 20 minutes for the flavours to come together.
2. Rub the steak with the olive oil, paprika, cumin, salt and pepper. Leave at room temperature for 30 minutes (or in the fridge for up to 8 hours).
3. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 15 minutes until tender, then drain.
4. Heat a griddle pan or barbecue until very hot. Cook the steak for 4–5 minutes per side for medium-rare.
5. Rest the steak for 10 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.
6. Spoon the chimichurri over the steak and serve with the potatoes and salad.

## Notes
- Slicing against the grain is the key to tender flank steak.
- Bavette or skirt steak work just as well.
- Leftover chimichurri keeps in the fridge for 3 days.
