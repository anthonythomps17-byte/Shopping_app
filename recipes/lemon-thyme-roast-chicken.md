# Lemon & Thyme Roast Chicken
Serves: 4
Time: 1 hr 45 min
Tags: dinner, sunday roast, family

A classic Sunday roast: a whole chicken with lemon, garlic and thyme, crispy roast potatoes, carrots and gravy.

## Ingredients
- 1 whole chicken (about 1.6kg)
- 1 lemon, halved
- 1 head garlic, halved across the middle
- 1 bunch fresh thyme
- 30g butter, softened
- 1.2kg Maris Piper potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 500g carrots, cut into batons
- 300g frozen peas
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### For the gravy
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 400ml chicken stock

## Method
1. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking. Heat the oven to 200C (180C fan).
2. Rub the butter all over the chicken, season well and stuff the cavity with the lemon, half the garlic and half the thyme. Sit it in a roasting tin with the rest of the garlic and roast for 1 hour 20 minutes, until the juices run clear.
3. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 8 minutes, drain and shake to rough up the edges. Heat the oil in another tin in the oven for 5 minutes, add the potatoes and roast for 50–60 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp.
4. Lift the chicken onto a board, cover with foil and rest for 20 minutes. Boil the carrots for 8 minutes and the peas for 3.
5. For the gravy, spoon off most of the fat from the chicken tin, put the tin on the hob and stir in the flour. Gradually whisk in the stock and bubble for 3–4 minutes, scraping up the sticky bits. Strain into a jug.
6. Carve the chicken and serve with the potatoes, veg and gravy.

## Notes
- Allow 20 minutes per 500g plus 20 minutes for any size chicken.
- Use the leftover chicken for sandwiches or a curry, and the carcass for stock.
