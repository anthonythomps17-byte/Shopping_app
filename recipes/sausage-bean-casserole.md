# Sausage & Bean Casserole
Serves: 4
Time: 50 min
Tags: dinner, one pot, comfort

A hearty one-pot of browned sausages, smoky beans and peppers in a rich tomato sauce.

## Ingredients
- 8 pork sausages
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes (400g)
- 1 tin butter beans (400g), drained
- 1 tin cannellini beans (400g), drained
- 250ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 crusty loaf, to serve
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method
1. Heat the oil in a large casserole pan and brown the sausages all over for 6–8 minutes. Take them out and set aside.
2. Add the onion and pepper to the pan and cook for 6 minutes until soft. Stir in the garlic, paprika and tomato purée for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, beans, stock, Worcestershire sauce and thyme. Return the sausages to the pan.
4. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook gently for 25 minutes, then uncover and cook for 5 more minutes to thicken.
5. Season to taste and serve with crusty bread.

## Notes
- Also good with mash or rice.
- Use veggie sausages for a vegetarian version.
